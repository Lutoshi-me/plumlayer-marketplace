# Abatement

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Third party air monitoring, inspection and final clearance | Monitoring | Excluded by every bidder because the abatement contractor cannot clear its own work, so say who you expect to buy it | any |
| The industrial hygienist | Monitoring | Most exclude the hygienist and a few carry the fee inside their own number, so say which you are doing | any |
| The hazardous materials survey | Surveys | Excluded by every bidder; name the survey and the issue of it your quantities came from | any |
| The survey's assumed material block | Quantities | Say whether material assumed behind walls and above ceilings is inside your number, and price it as its own add | any |
| Material that tested negative under a contaminated layer | Quantities | Say whether material that tested negative but sits under a contaminated layer is inside your quantities | any |
| Regulated material found once the walls are open | Unknowns | Excluded by nearly every bidder and priced as a change, so state the rates a change would be priced at | any |
| Material testing below one percent asbestos content | Regulated classes | Some exclude this by name; say whether it leaves as regulated waste or as ordinary debris in your number | any |
| Lead paint | Regulated classes | Say whether you carry lead as licensed abatement or under the occupational standard with the waste characterised by others | any |
| PCB caulking and sealants | Regulated classes | About half exclude these; state the depth of substrate either side of the joint in your base and the depth priced as an add | any |
| Mercury components and radioactive exit lights | Regulated classes | About half exclude these; say whether they are in your number or folded into universal waste | any |
| Universal waste: bulbs, ballasts, switches and thermostats | Regulated classes | Bidders answer this four different ways, so price it as its own line rather than inside the lump | any |
| Refrigerant recovery | Regulated classes | Carried by some as one stated mobilization and excluded by others; say which you are doing | mechanical equipment remaining |
| Demolition to access material on surfaces noted to remain | Access demolition | Contested and frequently unowned; say whether opening those surfaces to reach the material is in your number | any |
| Plaster ceilings at the deck and terracotta above ceilings | Access demolition | Say how these layers come off and whether their removal is in your number | existing plaster or terracotta |
| Mastic removal, levelling compound removal, grinding and shot blasting | Floor preparation | Nearly every bidder stops at the flooring, so price each step of substrate preparation by name or exclude it by name | any |
| Patching and preparing abated surfaces for new finishes | Patching | Nearly every bidder excludes it; say whether patching and preparing what you abate is in your number | any |
| Temporary power and water | Site services | Nearly every bidder excludes these; state the phase and the amperage per floor your containments need | any |
| Temporary protection, poly, barriers and weather protection | Protection | Most bidders exclude these; say what protection you carry and what you expect to be provided | any |
| Permits and fees, including sidewalk and overhead protection | Permits | Commonly excluded; name the permits you file and the fees inside your number | any |
| The regulatory notification and its fee | Regulatory | State the working day clock the filing runs on, the fee, and whether a notification is already open | any |
| The abatement work plan | Deliverables | Say whether the work plan and any non traditional work plan are in your number | any |
| Police and fire details | Site labor | Excluded by every bidder; state the details your own work needs so they are priced once | any |
| Slab scanning and layout | Site services | Nearly every bidder excludes these; say whether scanning before cutting is in your number | any |
| Shoring, bracing, lintels, scaffolding and staging | Access equipment | Nearly every bidder excludes these; price the staging your own work needs on its own line | any |
| Hoist and elevator operator time | Hoisting | Commonly excluded while dedicated use of both is required; state what you need, for how long, and who operates it | multi storey work |
| Additional mobilizations, overtime, premium time and second shift | Shift and sequence | Nearly every bidder excludes these; state the fee for each mobilization beyond the one you priced and the shift you priced | any |
| Salvage for owner reuse, its storage and reinstallation | Salvage | Most exclude salvage while claiming the scrap in the same document; say what you take away and what you set aside | any |
| Historic items, their protection and conservation | Historic fabric | Excluded in one blanket line by the bidders that mention it; price protection and salvage row by row | protected material |
| Work inside the occupied portion of the building | Occupancy | State that the occupied area is outside your number and price any work inside it separately | occupied building |
| Contaminated soil, storage tanks and free liquids | Site contamination | Excluded by every bidder; say who you expect to carry them | contaminated soil or tanks |
| Bond | Commercial | Nearly every bidder excludes the bond; quote the rate as a percentage of contract value at this project size | any |
