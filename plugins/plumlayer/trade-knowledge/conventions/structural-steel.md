# Structural steel

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Beam and beam-web penetrations for other trades | Penetrations and openings | Excluded from most steel bids; state a rate by penetration type and whether the work is shop or field | any |
| Cut, core and protect deck openings | Penetrations and openings | Excluded by fabricators, erectors and metal fabrications alike; state whether you carry it | any |
| Install furnish-only steel: egress beams, elevator beams, lintels, cap plates | Installation gaps | Furnished by one package and installed by neither; state which of these you set | any |
| Set and grout anchor bolts, leveling plates, leveling nuts and bearing plates | Installation gaps | Furnished by the steel package and set by nobody; state who sets and grouts before erection starts | any |
| Furnish and weld hanger tabs for deck | Installation gaps | Commonly excluded and picked up by nobody; state whether the tabs are in your price | any |
| Install deck on light gauge framing | Installation gaps | Deck on light gauge is a separate custody answer; state whether you install it | light gauge framing |
| Connections and hardware for steel attached to wood | Wood framing interface | Excluded by steel and often by the carpenter too; state which connections to wood you carry | wood framing over a podium |
| Plumb free-standing columns supporting wood framing | Wood framing interface | Necessary and rarely named; state whether final plumbing of these columns is in your price | wood framing over a podium |
| Anchor bolt, as-built and plumbness surveys | Surveys | Excluded by every steel bidder; state whether you survey or work to another package's survey | any |
| Field touch-up of shop primer and all finish painting | Finishes | Excluded by fabricators and by painters alike; state who touches up after erection | any |
| Furnish and erect architecturally exposed structural steel | Finishes | Excluded wherever it appears; state whether you carry the exposed members and to what finish | exposed structural steel drawn |
| Fireproofing, and its removal and replacement at erection interferences | Fireproofing | Excluded by every steel bidder; state who removes and replaces it around your interferences | any |
| Roof perimeter safety cable, and maintaining and removing floor cable | Safety | Floor cable goes in and stays; state who maintains it, who removes it and who runs the roof cable | any |
| Six foot fall protection | Safety | Excluded unless a numbered row asks for it; answer the row and price it where you carry it | any |
| Uniformed fire watch and police details | Safety | The market splits on this; state whether a dedicated watcher is inside your number | any |
| Thermal breaks where steel crosses the envelope | Envelope | Excluded by name and carried by nobody; state who supplies and installs them | any |
| Web doubler plates and the member upsizing your connections create | Connections | Where you design the connections, carry the doublers and the upsizing that follow from them | delegated connection design |
| Connection engineering and the professional stamp | Connections | State separately whether the engineering and the stamp are both inside your number | any |
| Masonry lateral support, ties and seismic clips | Masonry interface | Routed several ways and sometimes carried by nobody; state which of these you carry | masonry |
| Certification of the fabricator, the erector and the detailer | Certification | State which certifications each of you holds | certified shop required |
| Sales tax | Commercial | State whether sales tax is inside your number | any |
| Winter productivity on erection | Commercial | State how a December to March erection window changes your installation price | erection crosses winter |
| Local hire and workforce compliance goals | Commercial | State what you can commit to rather than answering best effort | local hire goals in the documents |
| The erector's obligations on the fabricator | Split package | Where fabrication and erection are bought apart, tie-off holes, cable stanchions, field bolts, bracing and delivery in erection sequence land on the fabrication side | fabrication and erection bought apart |
| Verify existing conditions and field measure | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder; state who verifies before you fabricate | renovation |
| Expose encased steel, remove coatings and slag, prepare for attachment | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder and it has to happen before you start | renovation |
| Remove and reapply existing fireproofing | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder; state who removes and reapplies it | renovation |
| Lead testing and abatement on existing steel | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder; state who tests and abates | renovation |
| Shore and protect the existing structure | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder; state who shores and protects | renovation |
| Scan existing concrete before drilling | Existing conditions | Excluded by every renovation bidder; state who scans | renovation |
