# Painting

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Exterior painting beyond hollow metal doors and frames | Exterior | Nearly half of painters exclude exterior work by name; state every exterior surface you carry, including cladding, trim, railings and bollards | any |
| Garage perimeter walls, ceilings and floors | Garage | Most painters exclude the garage unless the scope names it; price the perimeter, ceiling and floors as their own line | garage |
| Garage line striping and parking markings | Garage | Striping is excluded by nearly every painter; state whether you carry parking markings and at what coat count | garage |
| Wallcovering termination trims, reglets and outside corner trims | Wallcovering | Carried by nobody by default and installed before the wallcovering goes on; state whether you furnish and install the trims | wallcovering marks |
| Receiving and storing owner furnished wallcovering | Wallcovering | State whether you carry receiving, storage, adhesives and the quantities the owner has to order | owner furnished wallcovering |
| Wallcovering primer and its cure before hanging | Wallcovering | State the primer the wallcovering needs, its cure time before hanging, and who applies it | wallcovering marks |
| Go-backs and punch | Punch and turnover | No painter carries a go-back allowance by the hour; state yours as hours per unit or as a visit count and say which | any |
| Caulking where only one side is painted | Caulking | Painted to painted joints are the usual limit; state your maximum joint width and who carries unpainted, fire, acoustical, sanitary and exterior caulking | any |
| Intumescent and fire rated coatings | Specialty coatings | About a quarter exclude these by name and almost nobody carries them; state whether the coating and its material are in your price | rated coating shown |
| Photoluminescent egress markings | Markings | Carried by no painter; state whether you furnish and install the markings or who does | egress markings shown |
| Pipe identification, banding, stenciling and room numbering | Markings | Excluded by most painters; state whether you carry any of it where it is shown as painted graphics | any |
| Temporary heat and enclosure for coating conditions | General conditions | Excluded by the few who mention it; state the temperature and humidity your coatings need and who provides them | any |
| Off hours, overtime and unscheduled remobilization | General conditions | State how many mobilizations are in the price and your rate for each additional one | any |
| Substrate repair where existing wallcovering is removed | Preparation and touch-up | Removing existing wallcovering leaves substrate no package carries; state whether repair and skim coating are in your price | conversion |
| Touch-up of damage caused by other trades | Preparation and touch-up | About half exclude repair of other trades' damage; state what touch-up before turnover is in your number | any |
