# Flooring

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Self-leveling and floor leveling over concrete | Floor preparation | Excluded from finish flooring base bids beyond a skim coat; state the leveling you carry and the rate for anything past it | any |
| Floor preparation beyond a skim coat | Floor preparation | State the prep you carry and the rate per man hour plus materials for prep beyond it | any |
| Floor preparation over gypsum underlayment | Floor preparation | State the prep over gypcrete you carry and the hourly rate for prep beyond it | gypcrete substrate |
| Moisture testing of the substrate | Moisture | Excluded by nearly every flooring bidder; state whether you test or price against results handed to you | any |
| Moisture mitigation and pH correction | Moisture | Excluded by nearly every flooring bidder and an alternate for a few; price slab on grade and elevated slab separately | any |
| Sound and acoustic underlayment | Underlayment | Commonly excluded and priced apart from the finish; state the product, the thickness and the areas you carry, or say it is out | any |
| Sealer over gypsum underlayment, install | Underlayment | The underlayment contractor furnishes the sealer; state whether you install it ahead of your own work | gypcrete substrate |
| Protection of finished flooring | Protection | Carried by about half of bidders and an alternate for the rest; state the protection product per finish and how long it stays down | any |
| Flooring mockup | Mockups and testing | Price the mockup as its own named unit cost rather than folding it into the base | any |
| Attic and maintenance stock | Attic stock | State the percentage you carry for each material family and show it as its own line | any |
| Hoisting and material handling | Site and access | State hoisting as its own number rather than assuming free use of hoists and elevators | any |
| Debris removal beyond a central location | Site and access | Clean to a central location; the dumpster and the haul-off are the general contractor's | any |
| Final cleaning of the finished floor | Cleaning | Excluded by nearly every flooring bidder; final washing, waxing, sealing and vacuuming goes to final cleaning | any |
| General liability where no owner-controlled program applies | Insurance and bonds | Base the bid on an owner-controlled program and show conventional general liability as a separate add | any |
| Payment and performance bond | Insurance and bonds | Quote the bond as a percentage of contract and state whether it runs through the warranty period | any |
| Project portal and payment application fee | Insurance and bonds | State the portal fee as a percentage of contract with its cap, plus the flat amount per sub-tier sub | any |
| Resinous flooring and sealed concrete | Specialty floor coatings | Name the concrete and resinous marks you carry and the ones you do not, rather than listing them at zero | any |
| Unit entry thresholds | Transitions | Carry the thresholds in the base and price the prep at the threshold on its own line, or name them as excluded | any |
| Tile backer board and cement board | Tile | Offered only as an alternate by most tile bidders; state whether it is in the base and who installs it | tile in package |
| Shower pans | Tile | Supplied and set by others in most tile bids; state that you tile to the pan and price install only if you offer it | tile in package |
| Metal wall base | Wall base | Excluded by some bidders and often left unassigned; state whether it is in your number | metal base marks scheduled |
| Removal of existing flooring, adhesive and curing compounds | Existing conditions | Excluded by flooring bidders; take-up and contaminant removal go to demolition | renovation |
