# Miscellaneous metals

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Concrete fill at stair treads and landings | Stairs | Excluded from every metals base bid; by the concrete trade, confirm who carries it | metal pan stairs |
| Thermal breaks at relieving angles and balcony connections | Masonry support | No metals bidder seen carries them; state who supplies the bracket and who installs it | any |
| Installation of relieving angles and loose lintels | Masonry support | Fabricators supply only and the mason installs, on the mason's staging and schedule | masonry veneer |
| Layout of embeds, anchor bolts and leveling plates | Embeds | Metals furnishes and the concrete trade sets, but layout is claimed by nobody; confirm the split | any |
| Installation of prefabricated balconies and aluminum railings | Balconies and railings | Manufacturers supply only, so installation is priced as its own row by an erector | supply only quote |
| Installation of ballasted roof guardrail | Roof | The supplier furnishes and the roofer installs, because the rail bears on the roof assembly | ballasted roof guardrail |
| Hoisting, crane, lifts and staging | General conditions | Excluded by most metals bidders; confirm who provides hoisting, lifts and staging | any |
| Mock-up frame and mock-up pad | Mock-ups | Excluded by metals bidders; confirm who builds the frame and pours the pad | mock-up required |
| Access into the elevator shaft for elevator metals | Elevator metals | Nobody scaffolds the shaft; confirm access for pit ladders, sill angles and divider beams | any |
| Dunnage and support framing for generator and mechanical equipment | Equipment support | Excluded by most and carried as an allowance by some; confirm who provides it and its delegated design | any |
| Roof anchors and fall protection | Roof | Excluded by nearly every fabricator and bid by anchor manufacturers; confirm who carries it | any |
| Sign supports and sign stanchions | Signage | Excluded by metals bidders; confirm who supplies and installs the supports | exterior signage |
| Fire watch | General conditions | Separately priced by most and excluded by default by several; state whether dedicated fire watch is carried | field welding or cutting |
| Stamped calculations and shop drawings | Delegated design | Priced as named adds rather than folded in; state what carries a stamp and what does not | any |
| Demolition, disposal and temporary shoring at existing metal | Existing conditions | Cutting is carried and disposal and shoring are excluded; confirm who carries each | renovation |
| Louver support steel, angles, clips and fasteners | Louvers | Louver manufacturers supply the louver only and state a design load; confirm who makes the supports | louvers |
| Field touch-up of shop primer and of field welds | Finishes | Excluded by every metals bidder and commonly excluded by painters too; confirm who carries touch-up | any |
| Waterproofing at railing and balcony attachments | Waterproofing | Excluded by every aluminum supplier seen and a real leak path; confirm who flashes and seals them | balconies or roof mounted railings |
| Caulking and sealant at rail bases | Sealants | Excluded by most metals bidders; confirm who seals rail bases and rail penetrations | railings |
| Trash chute angles | Trash chutes | Excluded by some and carried furnish only by others; confirm who supplies and installs them | trash chute |
| Expansion joint covers and frames | Specialties | Excluded by some metals bidders and frequently unassigned; confirm who carries them | expansion joints |
| Corner guards, edgings, dock edge angles and wheel guards | Specialties | Excluded by some metals bidders and frequently unassigned; confirm who carries them | any |
| Countertop, cabinet and toilet partition supports | Millwork support | Excluded by most metals bidders and frequently unassigned; confirm who supplies and installs them | any |
| Site metal: fencing, gates, gate hardware, tree guards, bollards, catenary posts | Site metal | Some carry it and more exclude it; state whether site metal is in this number or the landscape package | site metal on the landscape drawings |
