# Window treatments

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Power and control wiring for motorized treatments | Motorization | Excluded from every window treatment number; the electrician carries motor power, the control station and any low voltage run, so confirm who wires what | motorized treatments |
| Blocking and backing for shade brackets | Rough openings | Excluded from every base bid; framing or drywall carries it, so state what backing you need and where | any |
| Ceiling pockets, soffits and recesses that conceal a shade | Ceilings | Left out of every shade number by name or by silence; drywall builds the pocket, so state the pocket the mount you priced needs | recessed or pocket mount |
| Flame certificate application and testing | Compliance | Excluded or unmentioned; state whether you apply for the certificate and test, and what that costs | any |
| Attic stock shades | Closeout | Named out by some and folded into the count by others; state how many spare shades are in your number and where they are delivered | any |
| Shade training and turnover instruction | Closeout | Left out of most numbers; include the turnover walk and the instruction session in the base and say how long it runs | any |
| Draperies | Drapery | Excluded where no locations or specifications appear on the set; say whether your number carries drapery or the owner's furniture package does | drapery on the project |
| Amenity and common area treatments | Amenity | Left out by bidders quoting apartment units only; price amenity and common area treatments as their own lines, separate from the unit shade count | amenity treatments |
| Escalation and storage if the schedule slips | Schedule | Excluded; state how long the price holds, and who stores material that arrives before the walls are finished | any |
| Overtime, off-hours, night and weekend work | Schedule | Excluded from base pricing; state your rate and get written approval before working outside normal hours | any |
| Material distribution by elevator, stair carry and street unload | Site logistics | Excluded and billed hourly; state what unloading, hoisting and floor distribution is in the base price | any |
| Additional mobilizations beyond one per section | Site logistics | Priced on one trip per section with further trips chargeable; state how many mobilizations are in your number | any |
| Warranty beyond the manufacturer standard and one year on installation | Warranty | Excluded and priced on request; state the cost of the longer warranty the specification calls for | extended warranty specified |
