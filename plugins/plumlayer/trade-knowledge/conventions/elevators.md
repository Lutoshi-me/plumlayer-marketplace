# Elevators

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Operator time for other trades using the hoistway | Hoistway access | Carry ride time with a stated hours basis, since only a licensed elevator mechanic may run a car for another trade | any |
| Temporary use of the permanent car | Temporary use | No elevator base bid carries this, so price the startup and inspection, the running period, the operator and the clean down as separate parts | permanent car used before turnover |
| Cutting, patching, core drilling and firestopping at hoistway penetrations | Hoistway readiness | Excluded from every elevator base bid and it recurs at every penetration, so confirm who carries it | any |
| Hoistway barricades and full entrance screening | Hoistway readiness | Erect, maintain and remove to fall protection standards, since elevator bidders exclude it on new construction | new construction |
| Counterweight screening in a shared hoistway | Hoistway readiness | Must be complete before the elevator crew mobilises, and elevator bidders exclude it | counterweight inside a shared hoistway |
| Temporary working platform at the top of the hoistway | Hoistway readiness | Erect, maintain and remove it, and expect the electrician to work off it as well | any |
| Pit ladder | Pit | Answered three ways by elevator bidders, supply and install, supply only, or leave it entirely, so say which you carry | any |
| Grouting of sills and entrance frames | Hoistway readiness | Grout under and in front of the sills and around the machine beams once the frames are set, since most elevator bidders exclude it | any |
| Concrete belt or built in inserts at rail brackets in a masonry hoistway | Hoistway structure | Hollow block will not take bracket fastenings, so build the inserts in as the wall goes up or add a concrete belt at every floor | masonry hoistway |
| Structural stamp on the elevator shop drawings | Permits and approvals | Elevator bidders that mention the state permit stamp exclude it and no other package prices it, so name who stamps | any |
| Certification that the building carries the elevator loads | Permits and approvals | Elevator bidders supply loads and reactions and certify nothing, and a basement or adjacent machine roughly doubles the wall reactions | any |
| Seismic provisions to the project design category | Design gates | Most elevator bidders exclude these until the category is confirmed, so state the category you priced | seismic category unconfirmed |
| Confined space monitoring, attendants and rescue planning at the pit | Site conditions | Several elevator bidders exclude this where the pit is classified that way, so state what you carry | pit a permit required confined space |
| Storage area and delivery access for elevator material | Site conditions | Provide the dry, secure, enclosed footprint per car near the hoistway with rollable access at grade, or storage and remobilisation are billed | any |
| Refuse containers for elevator packaging | Site conditions | Provide containers for elevator packaging or the disposal falls to the owner | any |
| Dried in hoistway held dry for the whole installation | Hoistway readiness | Dry the hoistway in before the elevator crew mobilises, keep it dry, and hold the stated minimum temperature in the work areas | any |
| Crane time for setting elevator equipment | Site conditions | Answered three ways by elevator bidders, carried, required from the general contractor, or offered as an alternate, so state which | any |
| Permanent power available for the whole installation | Electrical readiness | Traction installations typically need permanent characteristic power for the entire installation, so state the date you need it | traction |
| Absorption of regenerated power on standby power | Electrical readiness | Most current machines push power back, so confirm the standby generator can absorb it or price the units that dissipate it | standby generator serves the elevators |
| Attic stock and spare parts | Closeout | Elevator bidders exclude these and no other package carries them, so state what you include | specification calls for attic stock |
| Flood zone equipment and provisions | Site conditions | Elevator bidders exclude these and they are frequently unassigned, so state what you carry | flood zone |
| Domestic sourcing, filed sub bid and local hiring compliance | Public work compliance | Most elevator bidders refuse these outright, so state plainly whether you can meet them | public work |
| Asbestos survey and abatement in the existing hoistway | Existing conditions | Excluded by every elevator bidder and it must be complete before the elevator crew works | modernization |
| Removal of non elevator pipes and wiring found in the existing shaft | Existing conditions | Excluded by every elevator bidder and found on discovery, so state the assumption you priced | modernization |
| Disposal of removed machines, controllers, ropes and hydraulic oils | Existing conditions | Excluded by every elevator bidder, which leaves it with the owner or the general contractor | modernization |
| Decommissioning of existing cars | Existing conditions | Name the operations you carry: the state decommission permit, pinning the hoistway doors, landing the counterweight, lowering the car onto the buffers, removing the hoist ropes and the state inspection | existing cars removed |
| Rigging route for the machine | Existing conditions | Price the access hatch with its ceiling removal and reinstatement and the services beneath it against crane picks, and say which you assumed | modernization |
