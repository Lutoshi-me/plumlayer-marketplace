# Roof anchors and fall protection

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blocking and backer plates at every anchor location | Structural support | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by the carpenter or the steel fabricator and engineered by the engineer of record, confirm who carries it | any |
| Certification that the structure takes the anchor load | Structural support | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by the engineer of record, and the bid documents often name nobody, so confirm who carries it | any |
| Flashing and sealing at every anchor penetration | Roof interface | Excluded from every anchor base bid and generally by the roofer too, sealants and isolation coating at dissimilar metals with it; confirm who carries each penetration | any |
| Thermal breaks and isolation pads at anchor bases | Roof interface | Not carried in anchor base bids; price the break or pad at each anchor base or name who does | any |
| Hoisting and unloading anchors and cable to the roof | Access and hoisting | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by the general contractor, confirm who carries it | any |
| Access to roof levels and a safe working platform | Access and hoisting | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by the general contractor, ladders, hatches and guardrails with it | any |
| The parapet or guardrail the anchorage compliance rests on | Code compliance | Excluded from every anchor base bid; the system can pass its own load test and leave the roof non-compliant without it, so confirm who builds it | any |
| Fall protection at the skylights | Code compliance | Excluded from anchor base bids and often unassigned; confirm who protects the skylight openings | skylights on the roof |
| Opening and closing ceilings at anchor connections | Renovation access | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by drywall where the connection is made from below, confirm who carries it | connection made from below |
| Removing and reinstating fireproofing at anchor connections | Renovation access | Excluded from every anchor base bid; by the fireproofing contractor, confirm who carries it | sprayed fireproofing at the connection |
| Certifying the system for use by the trades in construction | Testing and certification | Not in the base bid; price it separately where the trades will tie off before turnover, and expect a second load test at handover | trades tie off before turnover |
| Priming and painting the anchors | Finishes | Excluded from anchor base bids and often unassigned; confirm who primes and paints the products | anchors specified primed or painted |
| Notching catwalk grating at the anchors | Layout conflicts | Excluded from anchor base bids and unassigned as often as not; confirm who notches the grating | catwalks on the roof |
| A soil box where an anchor lands inside a planter | Layout conflicts | Almost nobody carries this; confirm who builds the soil box around the anchor | anchor inside a planter |
