# Overhead and coiling doors

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Line voltage power, conduit, disconnects and final connections at the operator | Electrical | Excluded from every overhead and coiling door bid; the electrical contractor runs the feed, conduit, disconnects and final connections | any |
| Fire alarm tie-in that drops a rated door or a smoke curtain | Fire alarm | No door bidder carries the tie-in or the detection devices; name the party that does before the numbers are compared | rated or alarm released door |
| Opening framing, jambs, lintels, hood and spring supports, saw cutting | Opening preparation | Excluded from every overhead and coiling door bid; the opening and its supports come ready or nobody prices them | any |
| Field painting and finishes at the door opening | Painting | Excluded from every overhead and coiling door bid; confirm the painter carries the door, the frame and the opening | any |
| Warranty beyond the manufacturer's standard | Warranty | Standard manufacturer warranty only unless an extension is asked for; price any extension as a separate add | any |
| Access panels serving a door or curtain product above a ceiling | Access panels | Excluded by the door bidders; drywall furnishes and sets the panel, confirm who carries it | product above a ceiling |
| Bond and general liability | Insurance and bonds | Not inside the base number unless it is asked for; state bond and general liability cost as separate figures | no owner controlled insurance program |
