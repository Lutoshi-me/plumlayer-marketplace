# Low voltage

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Conduit, raceway, cable tray and flexible conduit | Pathway | Excluded from every low voltage base bid; say whether any pathway is in your number and where it stops | any |
| Coring, sleeving and firestop sleeves | Pathway | Say whether coring and sleeving is in your number, and whether it stops at the telecom room walls | any |
| Work, junction, floor and poke-through boxes | Pathway | Say whether the boxes at your outlets and devices are in your number | any |
| Firestop at penetrations outside the telecom rooms | Firestopping | Excluded by essentially every low voltage bidder; say whether any certified firestopping is in your number | any |
| Line voltage power and its connections | Power | Excluded from every low voltage base bid; say whether any permanent or temporary power connection is in your number | any |
| Grounding backbone and bus bars | Grounding | Some carry this and some do not; say plainly whether the grounding backbone and the bus bars are in your number | any |
| Plywood backboards in the telecom rooms | Telecom rooms | Excluded by every low voltage bidder seen; say whether the fire rated backboards are in your number | any |
| Service provider requirements for the telecom rooms | Telecom rooms | State the room sizes, cooling, sleeves, backboards and entrance conduit your number assumes, since these land on other packages | managed internet service provider |
| Cutting, patching and painting | Patching | Say whether cutting, patching and painting behind your work is in your number | any |
| Door preparation and door hardware | Doors | Say whether preparing the doors and frames for readers, contacts and electrified locks is in your number | any |
| Fire alarm relay modules | Fire alarm | Say whether the relay modules are in your number where fire alarm is bid by another package | fire alarm bid separately |
| Active network switches and electronics | Owner equipment | Excluded by every low voltage bidder seen; say what you expect the owner's information technology vendor to furnish, including internet addressing | any |
| Uninterruptible power supplies and power distribution units | Owner equipment | Say whether the uninterruptible power supplies and the power distribution units are in your number | any |
| Utility fiber to the property line and the service provider's work | Service provider | Say where your fiber stops and what you expect the service provider to bring to the building | any |
| Audio visual work | Systems boundary | Excluded by name by most low voltage bidders; say whether any audio visual work is in your number | any |
| All work inside the living units | In-unit work | State whether the work inside the living units is in your number, and price it on its own line | residential |
| Equipment furnished by a third party integrator | Equipment supply | State whether you furnish the security equipment or install equipment furnished by another party | any |
| Camera network cabling and the head end termination | Surveillance | State whether the cabling to the cameras and its termination at the head end is in your number | any |
| Access credentials | Access control | State the credential count in your number, since most numbers carry none | any |
| Lifts | General requirements | Say whether lifts are in your number or you expect them on site | any |
| Debris removal to the chutes or dumpsters | General requirements | Say whether removal of your debris to the chutes or dumpsters is in your number | any |
| Payment and performance bonds | Commercial | State the bond rate against each base bid you carry rather than folding it into the base | any |
