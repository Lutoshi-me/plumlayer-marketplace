# Smoke curtains

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Line and low voltage power to the curtain controller | Electrical | Not in any curtain price; conduit, wiring, boxes, disconnects and hookups are by the electrical contractor | any |
| Fire alarm tie-in that drops the curtain | Fire alarm | Not in any curtain price; the detection and the signal that drops the curtain are by others, and who carries them is often unsettled, so confirm it | any |
| Blocking, backing and supports for the head box and guides | Blocking and backing | Not in any curtain price; the framing or drywall package carries the structural support | any |
| Soffit, ceiling work and access panels at the curtain | Drywall and ceilings | Not in any curtain price; the soffit that conceals the head box and the panels that give service access are by the drywall package | concealed or recessed curtain |
| Cutting the elevator lobby finish to receive the guides | Elevator lobby finishes | Not in any curtain price; who cuts the stone, tile or millwork jamb to receive the guides is unsettled, so confirm it | stone, tile or millwork jamb |
| Receiving, offload and storage of the curtains at site | Site logistics | Curtain bidders assume the general contractor receives and stores the units; carry it | any |
| Permits and fees for the curtain work | Permits | Not in any curtain price; permits and fees are by the general contractor | any |
| Bond for the curtain package | Bonds and insurance | Not in the base price; state it as a separate figure | bonded project |
| Warranty beyond the standard one year | Warranty | Only the standard one year is carried; price any longer term separately | extended warranty required |
