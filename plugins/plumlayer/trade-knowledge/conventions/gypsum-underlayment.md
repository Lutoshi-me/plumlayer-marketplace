# Gypsum underlayment

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Installation of the furnished sealer or primer | Sealer | Nearly every bidder furnishes the sealer and excludes its installation; state whether you install it and the day rate if you do | any |
| Pre-pours at tubs, showers, mechanical closets and dead spaces | Pre-pours | Excluded from every base bid; state the count of pre-pour locations you carry and the price per occurrence | any |
| Trimming and removal of the excess perimeter isolation strip | Perimeter isolation | Excluded by every bidder; state whether you trim and remove the excess strip and the hourly rate if you do | any |
| Floor preparation after the pour and before the finish floor | Floor preparation | Excluded by nearly every bidder; state the surface you leave and what preparation beyond it you carry | any |
| Protection of the cured pour with temporary planking | Protection | Commonly excluded; state whether you furnish, install or maintain planking under wheeled and concentrated loads | any |
| Repair of damage to the cured pour caused by other trades | Protection | Commonly excluded; state your rate for repairing damage caused by others after your pour | any |
| Moisture testing and mitigation of the substrate | Moisture | Excluded wherever it is named and frequently unassigned; state whether you test or mitigate | any |
| Pour stops at stair, elevator and chase openings | Openings | Pushed to the general contractor by most bidders; state whether pour stops are in your number | any |
| Leveling to a specified floor flatness tolerance | Flatness | State the flatness your pour achieves and price leveling to the specified tolerance as a separate alternate | flatness tolerance specified |
| Underlayment at stairwells, stair landings and elevator lobbies | Excluded locations | Excluded by some bidders and frequently unassigned; answer yes or no for each location rather than staying silent | any |
| Underlayment at concrete areas, cellars, roof levels and parking | Excluded locations | Excluded by some bidders and a real gap on a mixed substrate level; state which portion of each level you pour | mixed substrate level |
| Curtain wall infills, expansion joint stops and grout at core joints | Excluded locations | Excluded by a minority and usually unassigned; state whether these are in your number | named on the drawings |
| Sealing of holes, voids and penetrations before the pour | Substrate preparation | Excluded by underlayment bidders; the floor is sealed against leakage before you pour, so state what you check on arrival | any |
| Subfloor patching, repair and deflection correction | Substrate preparation | Excluded by underlayment bidders; state the substrate condition and deflection limit you require before mobilizing | any |
| Sweeping and vacuuming the floor before the pour | Substrate preparation | Excluded by a minority and a precondition either way; state whether you clean the floor or arrive to a clean one | any |
| Firecaulking of the floor before the pour | Substrate preparation | Excluded emphatically by some bidders and it must precede your pour; state whether any of it is in your number | any |
| Heat, water supply, staging area and dumpster for the pour | Site and access | State the water flow and distance, the staging footprint and the temperature the building must hold before and after the pour | any |
| Hoisting and material handling to the poured floors | Site and access | Some bidders require a lull and driver from the general contractor; state hoisting as its own number | any |
| Permits, street rental, police details and traffic control | Site and access | Commonly excluded; state anything of this kind you carry rather than assuming it is provided | any |
| Removal of pallets, bags and excess sand | Site and waste | About half of bidders exclude pallet and sand removal; state whether it is in your number or the dumpster carries it | any |
| Waiting time and remobilization where the floor is not ready | Mobilization | State the notice you require per mobilization and your waiting and remobilization rates | any |
| Placement below the stated minimum temperature | Building conditions | State the minimum temperature you place at and how a winter pour date is repriced | winter schedule |
| Prevailing wage or union installation | Labor basis | State your labor basis and price prevailing wage or union installation separately where the project requires it | prevailing wage or union project |
| Local hiring and workforce requirements | Labor basis | Excluded by a minority; state the cost of meeting a local hiring requirement where one applies | local hiring requirement |
| Payment and performance bond | Insurance and bonds | Quote the bond as a percentage of contract value rather than folding it into the base | any |
| General liability where no owner-controlled program applies | Insurance and bonds | Base the bid on an owner-controlled program and show the conventional general liability add separately | any |
| Warranty beyond one year of workmanship | Warranty | One year is the market default; state the cost of the warranty term the specification asks for | warranty over one year specified |
| Acoustic engineering and sound design of the assembly | Acoustics | Rarely named by bidders and carried by none; state the rating your assembly is offered against | any |
