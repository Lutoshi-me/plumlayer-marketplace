# Earthwork and Sitework

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ledge, boulders and obstructions above the stated threshold | Obstructions and unsuitable material | Excluded above a stated volume by nearly every bidder, so give the unit prices for culling, removal and disposal below and above that volume | any |
| Over-excavation and replacement of unsuitable soil below design elevations | Obstructions and unsuitable material | Excluded by nearly every bidder and picked up by nobody else, so give a unit price for excavating and replacing unsuitable material | any |
| Uncharted utilities in the way, and their support, removal or relocation | Existing conditions | Excluded by nearly every bidder; say who supports, removes or relocates a utility no plan showed | any |
| Electrical and mechanical disconnects and make safe of existing services | Existing conditions | Excluded by nearly every bidder; the systems trades make the services safe before this crew mobilizes | existing services on site |
| Test pits at the abutting foundations and under the existing slab | Existing conditions | Priced in crew hours as its own small package; carry the test pit logs, since the logs are the deliverable | abutting structures or an existing slab |
| Backfill of the existing basement | Existing conditions | Say whether you infill with processed concrete, import fill or reuse the demolition material, since the three are very different numbers | existing basement |
| Removal of unknown tanks and their contents | Environmental | Commonly excluded; say who removes a tank found in the ground and what it costs by the each | existing developed site |
| Hazardous and contaminated material handling, transport and disposal | Environmental | Excluded by every bidder; state the soil classification you assumed for export and what your price does if it changes | any |
| Soil sampling, screening, characterization and environmental consulting | Environmental | Excluded by every bidder; the laboratory work that sets the disposal classification is bought by the owner | any |
| Compaction and density testing | Testing | Excluded by most bidders and bought from a testing agency; pressure testing and chlorination of the pipe stay with the installer | any |
| Winter conditions, frost removal, blankets, ground thaw and snow removal | Weather and schedule | Excluded by nearly every bidder in nearly the same words; price it where the work crosses a winter | schedule crosses a winter |
| Engineered dewatering: wellpoints, deep wells and effluent treatment | Dewatering | Bidders carry local sump pumping with onsite discharge only; price mobilization, monthly rental and demobilization for anything past a sump | groundwater above the excavation |
| Discharge permit, sampling and compliance filings for dewatering | Dewatering | Excluded by nearly every bidder; price the permit, the pre-characterization sampling and the recurring sampling with the system | engineered dewatering |
| Storm water pollution prevention plan and its regulatory notices | Erosion control | Bidders maintain the controls and exclude writing the plan and filing the notices; say who does both | any |
| Vibration, settlement, air, dust and noise monitoring | Monitoring and survey | Excluded by almost every bidder; price each kind as mobilization plus monthly rental | abutting buildings or streets |
| Preconstruction survey of the adjacent properties | Monitoring and survey | Excluded by earthwork bidders; the condition survey of the adjoining buildings is bought before anyone mobilizes | abutting properties |
| Survey controls, baselines and benchmarks | Monitoring and survey | Bidders lay out their own work from controls provided to them and exclude establishing those controls; say who sets them | any |
| Stamped or certified as-built drawings | Closeout | Bidders provide unstamped drawings and files; say who stamps the as-builts | any |
| Police and fire details | Street work | Bidders often carry details for their own utility work and not for export and import trucking; state which details are in your number | street work |
| Permit fees, deposits and utility connection or tap charges | Permits and fees | Bidders take permits and their fees in different combinations; list every permit, fee and deposit you carry and every one you do not | any |
| Temporary fencing, barrier, gates and impact attenuators | Site logistics | Most bidders exclude the logistics plan hardware while urban bidders carry it; say whether barrier, fence, gates and attenuators are in your number | logistics plan on an urban site |
| Water for dust control, with the hydrant meter, permit and backflow preventer | Site logistics | Dust control is priced assuming somebody else supplies the water; say who buys the meter, the permit and the backflow preventer | any |
| Pavement striping, markings and traffic signage | Paving | The piece most likely to fall out of a paving number; say whether striping, markings and signage are carried | paving in the package |
| Permanent bollards, railings, guard rails and site furnishings | Site furnishings | Excluded by most bidders though several price the bollards; say which of these you carry | any |
| Buoyancy calculations for the drainage structures | Precast structures | Excluded by the earthwork bid and priced as an extra by the precaster, so say who does the calculation that decides whether the structures float | high groundwater |
| Handholes, vaults and switchgear manholes | Site electrical | About half of bidders carry these and half exclude them; say whether the castings and setting them are in your number | any |
| Breaking into live electric and telecom manholes and duct banks | Site electrical | Excluded by earthwork bidders by name; confirm the electrician or the utility carries the cut-in and the coring | any |
| Pre-trench, working pad and spoils handling for the shoring contractor | Support of excavation | The shoring bid needs a cleared alignment and a stable working pad and prices neither; state the depth and the equipment you assumed | support of excavation |
| Underpinning of the abutting structures | Support of excavation | Excluded by nearly every bidder; say whether underpinning and its stamped design come from you or from the shoring specialist | abutting structures |
| Removal of the internal bracing that runs through the permanent concrete | Support of excavation | Rakers, walers and struts sit where the foundation walls go; price removing them on its own line | internal bracing |
| Support work for the ground improvement contractor | Ground improvement | Pre-trenching, the working pad, the daily support crew, cut-offs and spoils disposal are answered several different ways; price them line by line | ground improvement |
| Certified arborist, root pruning and soft digging at the root systems | Landscaping | Occasionally excluded and claimed by nobody, and the drawings show only a tree symbol; say who protects the roots | trees to remain |
