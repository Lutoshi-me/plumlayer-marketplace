# Roofing

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wood blocking, nailers, sleepers and equipment supports at the roof | Rough carpentry | Excluded from nearly every roofing base bid, so state whether you carry any blocking at all | any |
| Substrate board over the roof deck | Roof assembly | A minority of roofing bidders leave the substrate board out, so state whether yours is carried | any |
| Through wall, sill and shelf flashing, reglets and counterflashing | Flashing | Roofing metal terminates the membrane only, so confirm masonry or siding carries these | any |
| Cutting, coring and patching of the roof deck | Roof openings | Left to the penetrating trade by most roofing bidders, so confirm who cuts and who patches | any |
| Electronic leak detection | Roof assembly | Priced as an alternate rather than in base, so give the primer, the testing and the survey separately | leak detection specified |
| Pedestal pavers, protection sheet and paver sub-base | Overburden | Bought from a waterproofer or a paver vendor rather than from roofing, so confirm where your scope stops | terraces or paver areas |
| Fixed or ballasted rooftop guard rail | Fall protection | No roofing bidder carries the rail product, so price install only against a furnished rail or state the whole system as an add | permanent rooftop guard rail shown |
| Blocking, gusset and stiffener plates at anchor and davit attachments | Fall protection | Anchor vendors leave the supplemental plates at their attachment points out, so confirm who supplies them | roof anchors or davits shown |
| Winter conditions, snow removal, tenting and heating | Winter work | Roofing bidders leave winter conditions out, so state what cold weather work your number assumes | winter installation |
| Temporary roofing, dry-in and temporary covering of curb openings | Temporary protection | Left out by most roofing bidders, so state whether any temporary protection sits in your number | any |
| Rooftop mechanical wall and pipe chase housing and its cap | Rooftop enclosures | Not carried in roofing base bids, so confirm who builds the housing and who caps it | rooftop mechanical housing shown |
| Plywood sheathing at membrane terminations on vertical walls | Rough carpentry | Roofing systems need plywood rather than gypsum sheathing above about two feet of return, so confirm the wall carries it | membrane returns up a wall |
| Collector boxes and downspouts at scuppers | Rainwater | Roofing carries the through wall scupper and the rest of the rainwater path is split, so state where your scope stops | scupper drainage |
| Gutters, downspouts and splash blocks | Rainwater | Carried by roofing on some projects and by siding or site on others, so state which of them you carry | gutters or splash blocks shown |
| Roof access ladders and alternating tread stairs | Roof access | Bought from misc metals rather than from roofing, so confirm who supplies and who sets them | roof access ladder shown |
| Solar penetrations and future solar provisions | Roof penetrations | Left out by roofing bidders that name them, so state who flashes them and who holds the roof warranty afterwards | solar provisions shown |
| Roof demolition and abatement sequencing | Existing conditions | State whether your number carries roof demolition and how you will sequence around the abatement contractor | renovation |
| Parapet framing and sheathing modification | Rough carpentry | Left out by most roofing bidders, so confirm carpentry carries the parapet framing and its sheathing | renovation |
