# Rough carpentry

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Full time project carpenter for the job duration | Site carpentry | No framing bid carries a project carpenter, so state whether one is in your number and for how long | new build |
| Blocking outboard of the sheathing, barrier, gypsum or roof membrane | Blocking | Framing and siding bidders both exclude this blocking, so say plainly whether you carry it | any |
| Extra blocking at window sills after the units are installed | Blocking | State whether the sill blocking that supports interior trim after window installation is in your number | any |
| Blocking for interior trim, shelving, curtain rods and television mounts | Blocking | Say whether blocking for trim and finish attachment is in your number | any |
| Supports and blocking for brick relieving angles | Blocking | State whether the wood supports and blocking at relieving angles are in your number | masonry facade |
| Roof curb, sleeper, raft and dunnage blocking at mechanical equipment | Roof carpentry | Carry the roof blocking and sheathing at equipment supports or exclude it by name | any |
| Blocking for roof davits | Roof carpentry | Price davit blocking separately from davit installation | roof davits shown |
| Roof davit installation | Roof carpentry | Price davit installation as its own line, separate from the blocking | roof davits shown |
| Roof mechanical and line set screen walls | Roof carpentry | Include the screen wall framing with its insulation and thermal breaks, or exclude it by name | roof screen walls shown |
| Platforms and blocking over the roof membrane | Roof carpentry | State whether platforms and blocking above the roof membrane are in your number | any |
| Safety rails at the roof | Temporary work | Rails at the roof are separate from rails at the floors, so state whether they are in your number | any |
| Temporary stairs below the podium | Temporary work | State whether temporary stairs below the podium are in your number | podium structure |
| Temporary loading doors, their removal and remobilization | Temporary work | Price the loading doors with the later removal and the return trip to close the openings | any |
| Tower crane for the framing work | Hoisting | State whether a tower crane is in your number and what monthly rate you assumed | tower crane required |
| Wet set anchors, cast in place anchor bolts and embedment plates | Anchors | Anchors set before the pour are excluded by framing bidders, so confirm who sets them | any |
| Field applied window extensions and their blocking | Windows | Price field applied extensions separately from factory supplied ones | masonry facade |
| Sill pans, back dams, field mulling and expansion foam at openings | Windows | Each of these is a separate answer, so state which of them you carry | window installation in the package |
| Sealed truss plans, third party engineering and field inspection | Engineering | State whether sealed truss plans and independent field inspection are in your number | specification requires them |
| Framing premium at crawlspaces, terrace step downs and setbacks | Framing | Price the premium for these framing conditions over your base area rate | odd framing conditions shown |
| Furring at masonry walls and alignment between dissimilar wall types | Framing | State whether furring at masonry and alignment framing between wall types is in your number | masonry walls |
| Mock-up framing | Framing | Price the mock-up framing as its own line | mock-up required |
| Elevator frame-in and framing after trash chute removal | Framing | Price the late framing at the elevator and after the trash chute comes out | any |
| Miscellaneous materials, fasteners and conveyance | Materials | Carry a lump for miscellaneous materials, fasteners and conveyance or say it is excluded | any |
| Certified sustainable lumber and chain of custody documentation | Compliance | State whether certified lumber and its chain of custody documentation are in your number | certified lumber specified |
| Fire retardant treated plywood backer panels at telecom and electrical rooms | Blocking | State whether the fire retardant treated backer panels are in your number | any |
