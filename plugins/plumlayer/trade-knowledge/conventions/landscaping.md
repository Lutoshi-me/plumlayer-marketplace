| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Subgrade preparation, rough grading and compacted aggregates | Earthwork | Excluded by essentially every landscape bidder; state the subgrade elevation you need area by area, since the build-up depth differs | any |
| Everything below the setting bed under each paving mark | Hardscape | Say mark by mark whether the setting bed, the concrete base and the aggregate base are in your number, since this stack is split differently by different bidders | any |
| Removal and disposal of classified soils and buried obstructions | Earthwork | Excluded by most landscape bidders and rarely priced at bid; say what you assumed about the material you dig into | any |
| Electrical for bollards, planter lights and irrigation controls | Electrical | Excluded by essentially every landscape bidder; say whether any wiring, conduit, controls or circuitry is in your number | any |
| Plumbing and gas at irrigation, fire features and grills | Plumbing | Excluded by essentially every landscape bidder; say whether the point of connection, backflow, meter, gas runs and final connections are yours | any |

| Site lighting fixtures and festive lighting | Electrical | Excluded by nearly every landscape bidder beyond the bollard itself; say which fixtures you carry | any |
| Railings, guardrails and handrails at terraces and ramps | Misc metals | On the landscape legend and answered no by every landscape bidder; say whether any of it is in your number | any |
| Tree guards, tree grates and detectable warning strips | Misc metals | Drawn on the landscape sheets and answered no by every landscape bidder; say whether any of it is in your number | any |
| Movable site furniture | Site furnishings | Chairs, tables, lounge chairs and movie screens are answered no by nearly every bidder; say whether you carry them | any |
| Street and municipal curb at the landscape boundary | Site work | Excluded by name by some landscape bidders while the site package assumes it sits here; say which curb runs are in your number | any |
| Erosion control blankets and matting | Erosion control | Offered as an alternate rather than carried in the base by most; say whether it is in your base and at what quantity | any |
| Watering during establishment | Landscape maintenance | Most bidders do not mention it and water before the irrigation system is live is by others; say whether watering is in your number | any |
| Soil testing, weed fabric, root barrier and straw mulch | Planting | Each excluded by name often enough to ask; say which of the four are in your number | any |
| Loam, spreading of loam and amendment of on-site soils | Soil | Excluded in boilerplate by some bidders that price a soil mix in the same document; say whether the loam and the amendment of existing soil are in your number | any |
| Mock-ups called for by a drawing general note | Mock-ups | Called for in a general note on many sets and priced by nobody; say whether the mock-ups are in your number | mock-up called for |
| Coring and cutting installed pavers for penetrations by others | Hardscape | Excluded by some bidders while the access is always yours to allow; say whether coring and cutting after installation is in your number | any |
| Protection of finish paving after installation | Hardscape | Excluded by some bidders on a sequencing condition; say whether you protect the finished paving and what sequence you assumed | any |
| Tree protection fencing and pruning of existing trees | Site preparation | Excluded by some landscape bidders; say whether tree protection and pruning of the trees that stay are in your number | existing trees to remain |
| Winter conditions, premium time and off-hours work | General conditions | Excluded by essentially every landscape bidder; say what working hours and what season your price assumes | any |
| Permits, police details, engineering and bonds | General conditions | Excluded by essentially every landscape bidder; say which permits, details and bonds are in your number | any |
| Lift, hoist and craneage for landscape materials | Hoisting | Excluded by landscape bidders working over structure; say whether the lift, hoist and craneage are in your number | over-structure work |
| Insulation build-up over the waterproofing membrane | Green roof | Refused in printed exclusion lists and priced in breakouts by the same bidders; say plainly whether the insulation over the membrane is in your base | over-structure work |
| Overburden removal to investigate a membrane leak | Green roof | The costliest item on this list and excluded by many; say whether a no-fault removal and replacement obligation is in your number | over-structure work |
| Membrane testing and inspection before the green roof mobilizes | Waterproofing | Green roof bidders require the membrane tested, inspected and the area closed to other trades before they mobilize; say what you assumed | over-structure work |
| Fall protection, tie-off anchors and temporary guardrails at the roof | Site safety | Excluded by most green roof bidders; say whether the fall protection at the terraces is in your number | over-structure work |
| Roof access stairs and external scaffolding | Access | Excluded by most green roof bidders; say what access to the terrace levels you assumed | over-structure work |
| Engineering for structural loading and wind uplift over structure | Structural engineering | Excluded by most green roof bidders and frequently unassigned; say whether the loading and wind uplift engineering and any wind securement warranty are yours | over-structure work |
| Efflorescence removal from hardscape | Hardscape | Excluded by most green roof bidders; say whether cleaning efflorescence off the finished hardscape is in your number | over-structure work |
| Plant replacement where irrigation is turned off by others | Planting | Excluded by most green roof bidders; say what irrigation continuity your plant guarantee depends on | any |
