# Casework

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Install the unit kitchens, vanities and panels | Installation | Most casework is quoted supply only; state whether installation is in your number and on what labor basis | any |
| Offload and distribute the cabinets to the units | Installation | Excluded from nearly every casework bid; state whether you offload and distribute or leave both to the general contractor | any |
| Hoist the cabinets to the floors | Installation | Assumed to be the general contractor's and free of charge; state any hoist, lift or elevator your price depends on | any |
| Deliver and freight the cabinets to the site | Delivery and freight | Price delivery and freight on their own line; tailgate full truckload during business hours is the assumed default | any |
| Carry sales tax on the casework | Taxes and fees | State sales tax on its own line rather than folding it into the material | any |
| Carry the import tariff and duty on the casework | Taxes and fees | State any tariff or duty on its own line, including where you quantify it without carrying it | imported product |
| Supply and install the countertop support brackets | Countertops | Cabinet and stone bidders both commonly exclude these; state who supplies and who installs them | any |
| Supply the subtops and adhesives under the countertops | Countertops | Commonly excluded by cabinet bidders; state whether subtops are in your number | any |
| Supply the sinks and cut the tops for them | Countertops | State who supplies the sinks, clips and faucets and who makes the cutouts | any |
| Install blocking and backing behind the wall cabinets | Blocking and backing | Not carried by casework; confirm blocking is in and correctly located before the cabinets arrive | any |
| Frame, board and paint the die walls at islands and peninsulas | Blocking and backing | Not carried by casework; the die wall is by others and has to be finished before the cabinets land | islands or peninsulas |
| Supply the appliance panels and panel-ready fronts | Appliances | Excluded by nearly every cabinet bidder and rarely picked up elsewhere; state whether you carry them | panel-ready appliances |
| Supply and install closet, linen and laundry shelving and rod | Shelving | Commonly excluded by cabinet bidders; state whether shelving and rod are in your number | any |
| Supply the undercabinet and strip lighting at the wall cabinets | Electrical | Excluded from nearly every base bid; state whether the fixture and its moulding are an add and note the circuit is not yours | undercabinet lighting drawn |
| Supply the mirrors, medicine cabinets and bath accessories | Specialties | Carried by about half of cabinet bidders; state whether they are in your base or offered as an add | any |
| Supply and install crown, light rail and shoe moulding | Mouldings | Commonly supplied loose with installation by others; state whether you supply it and whether you install it | mouldings drawn |
| Protect the installed casework | Protection and cleanup | Rarely carried; state whether protection after installation and composite clean-up are in your number | any |
| Caulk the casework to adjacent surfaces and fill the nail holes | Protection and cleanup | Excluded by some supply and some install bidders; state whether caulking and nail hole fill are in your number | any |
| Cut the cabinets for plumbing after installation | Plumbing | Field cutouts at dishwashers and sinks are excluded by a minority; state who makes them | any |
| Build the casework mock-up unit | Mock-up | Excluded by nearly all; price the mock-up separately and state whether it may remain part of the finished work | mock-up required |
| Store the cabinets if the site is not ready | Storage | State your storage basis and rate per truck per week if the site is not ready when the product is | any |
| Supply attic stock and touch-up kits | Attic stock | Mostly excluded; state the counted quantity of attic stock and touch-up kits you carry | any |
| Supply certified, formaldehyde-free or fire retardant material | Material standards | Commonly excluded, and these are material substitutions rather than paperwork; state whether your quoted product meets them | certification required |
| Install with union or prevailing wage labor and certified payroll | Labor basis | State your labor basis and price the prevailing wage option where the project requires it | prevailing wage project |
| Carry the bond and the insurance program participation | Taxes and fees | State bond and any owner or contractor controlled insurance program cost as an amount and not only as a percentage | bonded project |
| Carry the payment platform, certification and processing fees | Taxes and fees | State any payment platform, certification seal or contract processing fee in your number | any |
| Pre-drill the doors and drawer fronts for the pulls | Hardware | A supplier that does not furnish the pulls will not pre-drill; state whether pre-drilling is in your number | pulls by others |
| Supply and install the amenity and common area casework | Amenity casework | Commonly excluded by unit casework bidders; list every amenity room you carried by its room number | amenity rooms drawn |
