# Final cleaning

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Additional cleaning passes beyond those bid | Passes | State how many passes your number carries; passes beyond them are not in the base | any |
| Reclean areas after trades return to finish work | Passes | Recleaning caused by work continuing after your clean is billed hourly by every cleaner, so pricing assumes the cleaner is last in | any |
| Clean exterior glass and provide the access to reach it | Glass and windows | Exterior glass is excluded unless your number names the access method, and the lift, davits or staging goes with it | any |
| Remove protective film from exterior windows | Protection | State whether film removal from exterior windows is in your number; it sits between the cleaning and glazing packages | any |
| Remove floor, wall and countertop protection | Protection | State whether removing floor, wall and countertop protection is in your number; cleaners answer this both ways | any |
| Remove excessive overspray, caulk, grout, silicone and adhesive | Surface cleanup | Excessive overspray, caulk, grout, silicone and adhesive removal is not in the base and is billed hourly | any |
| Wash the roof and terraces | Exterior areas | Roof and terrace washing is not in a cleaning base; price it as a named line | roof or terrace areas |
| Clean the sub-basement and other below grade levels | Areas covered | Name every below grade level your number covers; sub-basements are commonly left out | sub-basement or lower level |
| Clean owner furniture and equipment in common areas | Owner property | Cleaning owner furniture and equipment is excluded by cleaners; confirm who carries it | furnished common areas |
| Overtime, off hours and premium time cleaning | Labor conditions | Overtime, off hours and premium time are excluded from the base; state your rate for them | any |
| Assemble appliances delivered knocked down | Appliances | State whether appliance assembly is in your number; most cleaners do not carry it | appliances delivered unassembled |
| Dumpsters, water, power, toilets and permits for the crew | Site services | Dumpsters, water, power, toilets and permits are by the general contractor, not in your number | any |
