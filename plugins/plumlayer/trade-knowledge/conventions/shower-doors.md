# Shower Doors and Enclosures

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Set shower pans and make the drain connection | Plumbing | Excluded from every shower door, enclosure and surround bid seen; a licensed plumber sets the pan, which is made for a specific drain assembly the supplier does not provide | any |
| Blocking and backing at surround panels, doors and grab bars | Blocking | Carried by no shower door or surround bidder, by note code or by silence; the framing or drywall package carries it, confirm who | any |
| Install shower doors, surrounds and pans | Installation | A manufacturer that ships product does not install it, so name whether a certified installer, the supplier's own third party or the finish carpentry package carries the install | manufacturer supply |
| Receive, unload and store shower product on site | Site handling | Offshore-sourced suppliers exclude unloading, including the equipment and licensed operator, and note that surround crates need extended forks | offshore-sourced supply |
| Protect installed pans and finished glass | Protection | Pans go in early and live through the rest of the build, and almost no bidder prices protection, so confirm who carries it | any |
| Freight, crating and sales tax on shipped product | Freight and tax | Manufacturer quotes commonly leave freight and sales tax to be determined, so the printed total omits both; state them or say they are added at order | manufacturer supply |
| Tariffs, duties and import fees | Freight and tax | Offshore suppliers exclude duties by named category and pin their rates to a date, so state the exposure at the time of order | offshore-sourced supply |
| Waterproofing behind and beneath the shower assembly | Waterproofing | No shower door or surround bid carries the waterproofing behind the surround or under the pan; confirm which package does | any |
| Mock up, attic stock, hardware samples, bonding and hoisting | General requirements | Specialist enclosure bidders exclude all of these; state whether any of them is in the number | specialist enclosure supply |
| Off-hours install and deliveries | General requirements | Specialist enclosure bidders exclude off-hours work; state the assumption if the turnover sequence needs it | specialist enclosure supply |
