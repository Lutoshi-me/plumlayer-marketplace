# Trash chutes

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Rated shaft enclosure, fire wrap and fire caulking at the chute | Shaft and enclosure | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; the shaft is built, rated and sealed by others, confirm who carries the wrap and the caulking | any |
| Cut and frame the floor and roof openings the chute passes through | Openings and framing | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; the rough opening runs several inches larger than the chute diameter and that dimension has to reach whoever forms the slab | any |
| Roof curb at the chute vent, its framing, flashing and roofing | Roofing | Excluded from every trash chute base bid even where a curb is drawn, and often with a note saying so; curb, framing, flashing and the roofing around it are by others | any |
| Blocking and added support inside the chute shaft | Shaft and enclosure | Excluded from every trash chute base bid beyond the standard floor supports, and excluded by miscellaneous metals as well, so settle who carries the support frame before bid | any |
| Field wiring of the chute interlocks and compactors, and the power feed | Electrical | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; all interlock and compactor wiring and the power feed are by others, a low voltage interlock included | any |
| Sprinkler piping to the chute heads and its testing | Fire protection | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; the heads are furnished by the chute bidder and the piping to them and its testing are by others | any |
| Hot water, shutoff, backflow and floor drain at the wash down unit | Plumbing | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; hot water, shutoff and backflow serve the wash down unit and a floor drain is required in the discharge room | wash down unit shown |
| Air and duct sealing at the chute shaft | Shaft and enclosure | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; sealing air and duct penetrations at the shaft is by others | any |
| Smoke detector devices at the chute | Detection | Excluded from every trash chute base bid; the heat sensor is the chute bidder's and the detector device and its tie in are by others | any |
| Trash chute maintenance agreement | Closeout | Not carried in a trash chute base bid unless it is asked for; confirm who holds the agreement after closeout | maintenance agreement specified |
