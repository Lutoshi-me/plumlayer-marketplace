# Electrical

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Furnish the lighting fixture and controls package | Lighting | Say whether your price furnishes the fixtures and the controls or installs a package supplied by others | any |
| Furnish emergency inverters, drivers and fire rated fixture housings | Lighting | These ride with the fixture package and leave with it; confirm your price carries them | any |
| Receive, inventory and store owner furnished fixtures | Lighting | Where the package is delivered by others, say whether you receive it, store it and report damage and short counts | owner furnished fixtures |
| Temporary power, temporary lighting and temporary fire alarm | Temporary work | Answer these three separately; a temporary fire alarm rests on an approved impairment plan | any |
| Cut, cap and make safe the existing electrical | Demolition | Say whether you carry the demolition labor and the drop to the floor, and who removes it from the building | renovation |
| Utility company applications and back charges | Utility and providers | Say whether you carry the utility back charges or an allowance for them | any |
| Telephone and cable provider back charges and incoming services | Utility and providers | Say whether you carry the provider back charges and the cross connections | any |
| Concrete pads, pole bases, duct bank encasement and busway curbs | Concrete | Excluded from every electrical base bid; by the concrete or site contractor, confirm who carries it | any |
| Excavation, trenching and backfill for electrical conduit | Sitework | Excluded from every electrical base bid; by the site contractor, spoil removal included | site conduit |
| Cutting, patching and painting for electrical work | General conditions | Excluded by nearly all electrical bidders; confirm who patches and paints behind you | any |
| Roof penetrations, pitch pockets and their patching | Roofing | By the roofer, and several bidders ask for a bonded roofer; confirm who flashes your penetrations | roof mounted equipment |
| Furnish access panels for electrical openings | Access panels | Say furnish and install separately; about half of bidders carry the install only | any |
| Lightning protection system | Lightning protection | Say whether your price carries lightning protection or a specialty applicator bids it directly | project with lightning protection |
| Emergency responder radio system, band by band | Emergency responder radio | Answer fire, police and emergency medical separately; a band nobody carries is a gap | project with a responder radio system |
| Third party fire alarm testing, monitoring and infrared testing | Testing and inspection | Excluded by most; confirm who carries the third party testing, the monitoring and any independent testing | any |
| Third party firestopping of other trades penetrations | Firestopping | Electricians carry coring and firestopping for their own penetrations only unless they say otherwise | any |
| Putty pads, sound proofing and fire rated box enclosures | Firestopping | By drywall or a firestopping contractor; confirm who carries the pads at your boxes | rated assemblies |
| Crane and rigging the generator into final position | Generator | About half of bidders leave the rigging out; say whether your price sets the generator in place | project with a generator |
| Furnish wireless access points, switches and active network equipment | Network equipment | Say whether you furnish the active equipment or only install and terminate against equipment supplied by others | any |
| Furnish motor starters, drives, control relays and thermostats | Mechanical and plumbing equipment | Furnished with the equipment by the mechanical and plumbing trades; confirm who installs them | any |
| Equipment disconnects that arrive with the equipment | Mechanical and plumbing equipment | Read the equipment schedule note and say whether you furnish, install, or neither | any |
| Control and temperature control wiring at mechanical and plumbing equipment | Mechanical and plumbing equipment | Left out by nearly all electricians regardless of voltage; by the trade supplying the equipment | any |
| Appliance cords, pigtails and anti tip devices | Appliances | Say whether the appliances arrive with cords attached and who furnishes the pigtails | any |
| Install the microwave, hood and recirculating vent | Appliances | The electrician, the appliance installer and the casework installer each leave this out; say who you assume | any |
| Electrified door hardware, strikes, mag locks and power transfers | Door hardware | Hardware by the door frame and hardware contractor; price the box, the flex and the cable back separately | any |
| Apartment unit smart entry locks | Door hardware | Say whether you furnish the locks, program locks furnished by others, or leave them out | project with smart entry locks |
| Plywood and fire retardant backboards | Carpentry | Left out by most electricians; by carpentry or the general contractor, confirm who carries it | any |
| Duct smoke detector installation | Fire alarm | Detectors furnished by fire alarm and installed by mechanical; confirm the split | any |
| Fire alarm programming on an existing panel | Fire alarm | Say whether you carry the existing panel manufacturer programming cost by name or leave it out | renovation with an existing panel |
| Aspirating detection and pre-action system piping | Fire protection | Piping by the fire protection contractor; you provide the modules and the fire alarm connections | project with pre-action or aspirating detection |
| Hazardous material handling, ballast and lamp disposal | Abatement | Left out by most on renovation; by the abatement contractor, confirm who carries the disposal | renovation |
| X-raying of floors and slab scanning | Renovation | Left out by some on renovation; confirm who scans the slab before you core | renovation |
| Keep an existing tenant operational through the work | Renovation | Say whether you carry the off hours cutovers that keep an occupied tenant live | occupied building |
| Attic stock, spare parts and extra materials | Closeout | Left out by most and specified anyway; confirm you carry the specified spares | any |
