# Millwork

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Install, unload and distribute the millwork | Installation | State whether installation, unloading and distribution are in the number or priced as a separate option | any |
| Finished tops and subtops at millwork pieces | Countertops | Most millwork base bids leave the finished top out; state whether the top and the subtop are carried and by whom | any |
| In-wall support brackets and outriggers for cantilevered tops | Countertops | Left out by both the millwork shop and the stone fabricator, and they have to be set before drywall closes; confirm who carries them | any |
| Unit entry trim, transom panels and elevator transom panels | Trim | Almost always outside the millwork base; price them per each against the door and level counts | any |
| Framing and structure behind millwork pieces | Structure behind millwork | Die walls, planter carcasses, furred back walls, faux beams, fireplace surrounds and vanity framing sit outside the millwork base; confirm rough carpentry carries them | any |
| Amenity and common area baseboard | Trim | Falls between millwork and finish carpentry where the marks are not split by finish; state which marks are carried | any |
| Elevator cab interiors and cab handrails | Elevators | Left out of most millwork bids and not clearly the elevator contractor's; state who carries the cab woodwork and the handrails | any |
| Metal trims and reveals at millwork | Metal trims | Left out by millwork and often unassigned; state who supplies and installs the reveal marks | any |
| Felt, textile and acoustic wall and ceiling panels | Wall panels | Left out by millwork and often unassigned; state who supplies and installs them | any |
| Wall protection panels and fibre reinforced panels | Wall panels | Left out by millwork and often unassigned; state who supplies and installs them | any |
| Protection of installed millwork, and its maintenance and removal | Protection and storage | Millwork offers the first application only; state who maintains and removes the protection | any |
| Storage of fabricated millwork | Protection and storage | Priced by the truck week or the container month when the site cannot receive; state who carries it | site not ready |
| Shop drawings, engineering and field measurement | Submittals | State how many review rounds are included and who pays for further rounds | any |
| Trash and wire grommets | Hardware | Required by the woodwork specification and shown on no elevation; carry them in the base and state the count | woodwork specification present |
| Wood handrails, low wall caps and trims | Trim | Left out by some millwork shops and often unassigned; state who supplies and installs them | any |
