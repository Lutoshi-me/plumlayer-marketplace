# Glazing

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blocking and plywood behind mirrors, railings and enclosures | Preparation by others | Excluded from every glazing base bid; carpentry sets it before the glass goes up, so confirm who carries it | any |
| Slab chipping and the pocket for a floor closer box | Preparation by others | Excluded by glazing bidders that name it; concrete cuts the pocket before the door arrives | floor closers specified |
| Air and vapor barrier and membrane flashing at the storefront perimeter | Perimeter | Excluded by every glazing bidder; the envelope or waterproofing package carries it | any |
| Perimeter caulking at storefront and window framing | Perimeter | Say which beads you carry, exterior, interior or neither, because painters bound their caulking to painted surfaces | any |
| Glazing at hollow metal doors and frames | Openings | Price the lites by frame type and rating with a count, because silence here reads as scope you did not carry | hollow metal openings receiving glass |
| Louvers shown on the storefront schedule | Openings | Excluded by essentially every glazing bidder; confirm the mechanical package carries them | louvers on the storefront schedule |
| Entrance hardware at aluminum doors | Hardware | Price the hardware sets opening by opening, or state the allowance you carried where no hardware schedule exists yet | any |
| Automatic door operators and actuators | Hardware | State whether the operators and actuators are in your base and at which openings | automatic entrances |
| Wiring, power supplies and card readers at electrified openings | Electrical | Excluded from every glazing base bid; the electrical and security packages carry it before and after you | electrified or access controlled openings |
| Unit bathroom mirrors | Mirrors | State whether the unit mirrors are in your base and whether you priced them framed or unframed | any |
| Amenity, fitness and elevator mirrors | Mirrors | Name which mirror marks you carry, because ownership splits between glazing, specialties and the elevator supplier | any |
| Installation labor against a supply only offer | Installation | State plainly whether the price includes installation or delivers to the tailgate | supply-only offer |
| Shower enclosures | Enclosures | State whether the enclosures are in your base, with the count and the configuration you priced | project with glass shower enclosures |
| Glazing into wood framed glass partitions and office fronts | Partitions | State whether you glaze frames furnished by millwork, and whether doors and door hardware come with the glass | wood-framed glass partitions |
| The cap on a glass railing or guardrail | Railings | Excluded by glazing bidders that meet it; confirm whether finish carpentry or metals carries the cap | glass railings or guardrails |
| Delegated design and the professional engineer's stamp | Engineering | Price the delegated design and the stamp as a visible line rather than leaving it in the exclusions | delegated design specified |
| Field and laboratory performance testing | Testing and mock-ups | State whether performance testing is carried and at what stage | envelope-performance project |
| Mock-ups | Testing and mock-ups | State whether a mock-up is priced, since most glazing bidders exclude mock-ups of any kind | mock-up specified |
| Warranty term beyond one year | Commercial | State the warranty term you priced, because anything past the one year default is a priced adder | warranty past one year |
| Bond | Commercial | Price the bond as its own line and state the rate | bonded project |
| Final cleaning and washing of glass | Cleaning | Excluded by nearly every glazing bidder; the general contractor or the cleaning contractor carries it | any |
