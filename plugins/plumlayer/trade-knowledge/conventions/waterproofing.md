# Waterproofing

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winter conditions, tenting and temporary heat | Temporary conditions | Excluded from every waterproofing base bid; the general contractor carries enclosure and heat for cold weather work | any |
| Staging, lifts, swing staging and hoisting | Access | Say whether access is inside your number and how long it is there; about half of bidders exclude it and it is priced by duration | any |
| Sheet metal flashing, coping and sill back dams | Sheet metal | Excluded from nearly every membrane and barrier bid; confirm the sheet metal or roofing package carries through-wall flashing, counter flashing, coping and back dams | any |
| Preformed silicone extrusions and transition sheet | Sealants | Commonly excluded and often carried by nobody; confirm who installs preformed silicone at joints and transitions | any |
| Interior air sealing by aerosolized sealant | Air sealing | A separate specialty that no other package picks up; confirm who carries it | any |
| Third-party air leakage testing and infrared imaging | Testing | Excluded by every barrier bidder; the owner or general contractor buys it through a commissioning agent | air barrier in the package |
| Fire-rating inspection of expansion joints | Testing | Excluded by expansion joint bidders; confirm who buys the fire-rating inspection of the installed joint | expansion joints |
| Substrate preparation at foundation walls | Substrate preparation | Excluded near-universally; form-tie and snap-tie removal, cleaning, patching, parging, grinding and honeycomb repair go to the concrete contractor | cast-in-place foundation |
| Dewatering and water management during installation | Water management | Excluded by waterproofing bidders; the site or general contractor keeps the excavation dry | below-grade work |
| Damproofing at inner frost walls | Below grade | Small, easy to miss on the sheets and routinely carried by nobody; say whether inner frost walls are damproofed in your number | any |
| Mud slab or blinding under pre-applied membrane | Below grade | No proposal names it; say what surface the pre-applied membrane is laid on and who provides it | pre-applied membrane under a mat slab |
| Detailing at support of excavation tie penetrations | Below grade | Excluded while the membrane runs up against the support of excavation; those penetrations are the leak path, confirm who details them | blindside work at a support of excavation |
| Comebacks for below-grade pipe penetrations | Below grade | Some bidders exclude the return trip once penetrations are set; say whether a return mobilization is inside your number | below-grade pipe penetrations |
| Separate mobilization to waterproof footings early | Mobilization | Excluded by name; state the charge for a separate trip to waterproof footings so the site can backfill to footing level | early backfill at footings |
| Line striping at the garage | Traffic coating | Not carried by the traffic coating bidder; confirm who restripes after the coating goes down | traffic coating at a garage |
| Hazardous material removal and disposal | Hazardous materials | Excluded commonly and with an indemnity demand attached; an abatement contractor carries it | existing building |
| Mock-up | Mock-up | Price the mock-up as its own line; it is small enough to be dropped and it comes before production work | any |
