# Fireproofing

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Patch new fireproofing damaged by other trades | Patching | Excluded by essentially every bidder; carry it at a crew day rate and state the number of days | any |
| Repair and extend existing fireproofing to match | Patching | Excluded by essentially every bidder, including existing material outside a marked takeoff area; carry it at a crew day rate | renovation |
| Third party thickness, density and adhesion testing | Testing and inspection | Excluded by every bidder; the owner or the general contractor pays, so confirm who | any |
| Surface preparation and primer removal at coated steel | Substrate | Excluded by every bidder; where the steel arrives coated the lath or mechanical bond is added here | steel arrives primed or painted |
| The second fireproofing product where a bidder carries one | Fireproofing products | A bidder pricing one product has offered half the package, so buy the half it left out | both products required |
| Fireproofing at the underside of metal deck | Fireproofing scope | Excluded by nearly every sprayed material bidder and by name; confirm the test design does not assume it | metal deck |
| Overspray removal from decks and adjacent surfaces | Cleanup | Excluded or narrowed to cleaning below ceiling level only; confirm who scrapes the deck | any |
| Heat, temporary enclosure and ventilation for spraying | Temporary conditions | Excluded by every bidder; a minimum temperature is held a day or two either side of application | cold weather work |
| Power, water and hookups at the spray rig | Temporary conditions | Excluded by every bidder; a dedicated water source and single phase power are wanted at the staging location | any |
| Sealer or topcoat over intumescent | Finishes | Excluded by every intumescent bidder and carried by painters essentially never | intumescent |
| Mobilizations beyond the first | Mobilization | Excluded by nearly every bidder; state the rate for each additional trip and expect the schedule to demand them | any |
| Hoisting, dumpsters and debris removal | Site services | Commonly excluded; the general contractor carries it | any |
| Mockups | Mockups | Commonly excluded and frequently unassigned; confirm who carries it | any |
