# Countertops

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Plywood subtops, blocking and substrate under counters and at miters | Substrate | Excluded by nearly every countertop bid, so say whether you supply and set it or leave it to others | any |
| Honeycomb backing under thin porcelain tops | Substrate | Thin material needs backing that two and three centimetre stone does not, so state whether it is in your number | thin material |
| Support brackets and corbels at counter overhangs | Support | Excluded by nearly every countertop bid and needed on site before the stone, so confirm who carries it | any |
| Hoisting, lifts and vertical distribution of stone | Access | Excluded or made conditional on a hoist provided to you, so state what you assume | any |
| Sink supply | Sinks | Say whether the sinks are in your number priced by model or supplied by others | any |
| Sink setting into the cut opening | Sinks | Say whether you set the sinks or only cut and polish the opening | any |
| Sink clips, mounting hardware and dishwasher brackets | Sinks | Named by some bidders and excluded by others, so say which clips, brackets and strips are in your number | any |
| Support elements for cast iron or farmhouse sinks | Sinks | Some bidders exclude the blocking that carries a heavy sink, so confirm who builds it | cast iron or farmhouse sinks |
| Countertop protection film | Protection | State whether film over the installed tops is in your number and how long it stays | any |
| Kitchen backsplash in the material actually built | Backsplash | Kitchen splash is tile by others in most bids, so say what you carry if stone is wanted there | kitchen splash not in the tile package |
| Stone thresholds and saddles | Thresholds | Countertop bidders leave these to tile even where they carry stone marks, so confirm who supplies and sets them | stone thresholds scheduled |
| Caulking beyond the silicone joint at counter to splash and at sink | Sealants | Most bidders carry only the silicone joints they name, so say what other caulking is in your number | any |
| Scribing tops to walls | Installation | Some standing terms exclude scribing unless it is stated, so say whether it is in your number | any |
| Vein and bookmatch matching across seams | Fabrication | Excluded by about half of bidders and priced as an add, so state the cost if matching is wanted | vein or bookmatch matching wanted |
| Installation of the tops | Installation | Say plainly whether installation is in the number, because a supply only number is not comparable to an installed one | any |
| Receiving, offloading and distributing tops at the site | Access | Supply only bidders leave receiving and distribution to others, so state what you assume | supply only bid |
| Tariff, duty and freight escalation past the quoted rates | Freight and duty | State the rates you quoted, the date they hold to, and who carries an increase | imported material |
| Mobilization past the stated batch minimum, and on site storage | Delivery | State your minimum units per trip, your charge for an extra trip, and the storage space you need | any |
| Shop and coordination drawings | Submittals | Say whether shop and coordination drawings are in the rate or priced separately | any |
| Off hours, overtime and out of sequence work | Access | About half of bidders exclude premium time, so state the working hours your number assumes | any |
| Bond and added liability insurance | Commercial terms | State the bond rate and whether added liability applies where no owner controlled program covers the work | any |
| Second year of warranty past the one year default | Commercial terms | One year on installation is the market default, so price the delta where two years are required | two year warranty required |
| Payment portal fee | Commercial terms | Say whether a payment portal fee applies to you and at what rate | any |
