# Concrete

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Winter and hot weather provisions | Weather protection | Excluded by nearly every concrete bidder in both regimes, so state who buys heat, blankets, enclosures and cold weather admixtures | any |
| Testing, inspection and concrete cylinders | Testing and inspection | Excluded by every concrete bidder seen and bought by the general contractor or the owner, confirm who carries it | any |
| Housekeeping and equipment pads | Concrete pads | Excluded by nearly every bidder because size, location and quantity are not known at bid time, so carry an allowance or a unit price at an assumed area | any |
| Joint sealant and caulking at concrete joints | Joints | Excluded by nearly every structural bidder and picked up by nobody, so name who seals control, expansion and isolation joints | any |
| Curbing, cast in place, slip formed, precast or granite | Site concrete | Excluded by every full package bidder and routinely by the site bidder as well, so confirm who carries it | site work in scope |
| Site object footings for bollards, light poles, fences, benches and signage | Site concrete | Excluded by most site bidders and most structural bidders, so name each footing and confirm who sets it | site work in scope |
| Concrete washout and disposal of excess concrete | Site logistics | Excluded by most bidders, so state who provides the washout area and removes the waste | any |
| Bond premium and owner controlled insurance credit | Commercial terms | Held out of the base by nearly every bidder and priced on request, so ask for the bond rate and the insurance credit | any |
| Temporary shoring, reshoring and underpinning of existing structure | Temporary support | Excluded by most bidders wherever it is not the sequence they assumed, so state the shoring the new work requires | any |
| Duct bank and conduit encasement | Site concrete | Excluded by most concrete bidders and sent to site or electrical, so confirm which package pours it | duct bank shown |
| Safety railing and edge protection at elevated slabs | Safety | Carried by some bidders and excluded by many, so state whether the concrete package protects the slab edge | elevated slabs |
| Moisture mitigation, admixture or topical | Slab moisture | Excluded by nearly every concrete bidder and answered both ways by flooring, so name which mitigation is carried and by whom | any |
| Coated and specialty reinforcing | Reinforcing | Epoxy coated, galvanized, stainless, weldable and threaded bar are excluded by nearly every placer and supplier, so price them as their own line | coated or specialty bar shown |
| Setting of mechanical, electrical and plumbing sleeves | Embeds and sleeves | Carried by some bidders and excluded by others, so state who sets the sleeves and who furnishes them | any |
| Stair pan fill, nosings and cleaning the underside of stair pans | Stairs | Excluded by most concrete bidders and sent to steel or the general contractor, so confirm who fills the pans | steel stairs |
| Architectural and form-lined concrete finish | Finishes | Excluded by most bidders, who carry a plain as-cast finish only, so price any formed finish as its own line | architectural finish shown |
| Mockups and their removal | Mockups | Excluded by most bidders, so state who builds and removes the required mockups | any |
