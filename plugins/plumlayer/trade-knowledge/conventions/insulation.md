# Insulation

| name | category | note to bidder | applies when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cavity insulation at all framed assemblies | Cavity insulation | State whether the number carries the whole cavity scope; spray and single assembly bidders exclude every insulation but their own | any |
| Rigid insulation at slabs on grade and foundation walls | Rigid and continuous insulation | Excluded from every insulation base bid; bought as material and set by the concrete trade, confirm who carries it | any |
| Continuous rigid insulation at the cladding backup | Rigid and continuous insulation | Excluded from every insulation base bid; it rides the cladding backup and is bought in the siding package, confirm who carries it | any |
| Thermal or ignition barrier coating over exposed spray foam | Sprayed assemblies | The code requires the covering wherever foam stays exposed; name the coating product priced, or state where drywall is the covering | sprayed foam |
| Sound walls inside the dwelling units | Acoustic insulation | Bedroom, bath, laundry and mechanical closet walls are often not called out on the drawings; price them as a separate block and say so | any |
| Air sealing after the board is up | Air sealing | Bidders that carry sealing before rock exclude it; state who seals after the board | any |
| Foam at window and door perimeters | Air sealing | Carried in the base by some bidders and held as a priced alternate by others; state which this number does | any |
| Envelope airtightness testing and air barrier inspection | Testing and inspection | Nobody in this package carries it, and thermal imaging and whole building infiltration testing are excluded by name; state who tests | any |
| Ceilings at basement, podium and amenity levels | Ceilings | Bidders often carry only the ceilings above the dwelling units; state how far past the residential footprint this number reaches | podium or basement |
| Non combustible fill at interstitial floor cavities | Cavity insulation | No base bid carries it; price it separately where the floor cavities take fill | interstitial floor cavities |
| Firestopping at rated assemblies | Firestopping | Excluded by printed house terms unless listed, and sold elsewhere as a detachable option; confirm which model the project uses | any |
| Heat, containment and ventilation for sprayed work | Sprayed assemblies | Sprayed assembly bidders exclude it and work to a minimum substrate temperature; state who provides the heat | winter schedule |
| Substrate preparation and priming for applied assemblies | Sprayed assemblies | Sprayed and applied assembly bidders exclude preparation, priming and patching of the substrate; state who does it | sprayed assemblies |
| Spray stops at window and door openings before foam | Sprayed assemblies | Foam bidders require them in place by others before spraying; state who sets them | sprayed foam |
| Shop drawings for sprayed foam | Sprayed assemblies | Excluded by foam bidders; state who prepares them where the specification asks for them | sprayed foam |
| Manual sealing of penetrations before aerosol sealing | Air sealing | Aerosol sealing only closes what manual sealing left, and penetrations over about half an inch are repaired first; state who repairs them | aerosol envelope sealing |
