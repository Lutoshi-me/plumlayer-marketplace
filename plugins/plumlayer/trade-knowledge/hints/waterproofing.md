# Waterproofing

Air and vapor barrier: split rows by wall type. Masonry and concrete read as waterproofing; over wood and gypsum sheathing a panelized framer can carry it, so tag both.
Below-grade and horizontal assemblies enumerate by layer: membrane, protection board, drainage mat, rigid insulation at its thickness, wearing surface. Never one system row.
Below grade splits by method, never one foundation row: pre-applied at a support of excavation or under a mat slab, post-applied on formed walls, damproofing on frost walls.
Sealants split by joint condition, interior apart from exterior. The painting and tile packages reach into caulking at plumbing fixtures, countertops and stair-to-wall joints, so tag both.
Sheet metal flashing, through-wall flashing, counter flashing, coping and metal back dams are not waterproofing. Tag roofing or sheet metal, and tag both where neither is named.
Expansion joints split by location family and joint size. A firewall cover goes to drywall, a roof cover to roofing, and the fire barrier behind a cover is its own row at several times its length.
Balconies collapse to a count times a system. Railing embeds, flashings, door pans and transition pieces are metal and leave this package.
Amenity deck and plaza membrane over occupied space is waterproofing; the roof membrane and its parapet flashing stay with roofing. Tag both at the parapet.
Elevator pits get a row per pit with the count and car configuration, and say whether treated from outside with sheet or from inside with crystalline.
The strip from below-grade membrane up to the wall barrier at the top of the foundation is its own dimensioned row. Both sides stop there.
Traffic coating splits by location: garage entry, speed ramp, loading dock, parking deck. It reads to waterproofing wherever the deck membrane does.
Barrier transitions get a row each: corners, floor to exterior wall, wall to low roof, slab on grade to wall, wall to parapet top, courtyard to wall, and head, jamb and sill of openings.
Primer, adhesive, mastic, termination bar and seam treatment ride the membrane row.
On an existing building rows become an action against a surface: repoint, rebuild, rout and seal, patch, coat, clean, reanchor. One joint can carry two actions.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects, three multifamily and one renovation; not re-mined.
