# Painting

Wallcovering is painting's, furnish and install. Tag every wallcovering mark to painting and open the reflected ceiling plans, since marks sit on ceilings too.
Level five, skim coating and wall patching are drywall's. Painting carries nail hole, crack and dimple filling only; tag a finish level note to drywall.
Give each finish mark that is not plain paint its own row: wallcovering, wall protection, specialty coating, sealed floor, decorative. Plain paint rides the area row.
Split by area or room type: units, corridors, lobby and amenity, back of house, stairwells, garage, basement, penthouse, retail, exterior.
Walls, ceilings, soffits, columns and bulkheads inside one area collapse to one row; do not split them by element.
Split again by substrate where the coating changes: gypsum board, masonry, concrete, hollow metal, wood, metal stairs, exposed structure, exposed mechanical.
Doors, frames and trim are their own group, wood split from hollow metal. Wood doors are painted all six sides; write it on the row.
Where a note says prefinished or factory finished, tag doors, trim, millwork, wood base and cladding to their supplier, not painting; if the set is silent, tag both.
A bare windows row is ambiguous: sills and aprons ride it with the sash out, and jambs can be gypsum board returns. Write which you took.
Concrete floor sealer at back of house and epoxy at bike and pet rooms are contested with flooring. Tag both; the product the specification names decides it.
Exposed pipe, duct, conduit and deck are painting's where the finish schedule marks the exposed ceiling; where it does not, tag both painting and mechanical.
Acoustic, textile, felt and panel marks sit beside paint marks and can read as wallcovering. Tag both painting and drywall and ask.
Painted MDF and wood closet and linen shelving is contested with millwork and casework. Tag both.
Split caulking by joint condition: door trim to wall, window to wall, base to wall, counter to wall, stair stringer to wall. Carry the maximum joint width.
Levels collapse to a level range unless the coating changes; rooms collapse to room type on new construction, not on a renovation phase.

coverage: derived from a book mined in a fan-out over twenty-two projects in one market; derived here, not re-mined.
