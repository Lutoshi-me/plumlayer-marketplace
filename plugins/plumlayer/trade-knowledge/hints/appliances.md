# Appliances

The supplier stops at the appliance end: tag the receptacle, the hardwire, the valve, the water box, the duct and the gas line to electrical, plumbing or hvac, and the appliance here.
Range hoods and over the range microwave hoods are appliances, not hvac; tag the hood here and the duct, the cutting and the final connection to hvac.
Garbage disposals are supplied here and installed by plumbing; the sink top air switch is supplied here and wired by electrical.
Washers, dryers, stacked pairs, laundry centers and all in one combination units are appliances, not plumbing; the laundry pan and the shutoff box are plumbing, set before the appliances arrive.
Panel ready appliances are this package and their panels and handles are millwork or casework; give each panel ready appliance its own row so the seam is visible.
Collapse apartments to unit type and split unit type wherever the appliance set changes; accessible units earn their own rows and commonly take a cooktop and a wall oven in place of a range.
Key every row to the schedule mark and let the model number ride the row; the mark holds across a change of manufacturer and the model number does not.
Carry the count on every mark row; bidders reading one set report different counts, and a row without its count hides the disagreement.
Amenity and common area appliances are this package: private dining, coffee bar, club lounge, work room, package room, mail and trash area, fitness; one row a room and a mark.
Accessories each take a row: cords, water lines, ice maker connectors, fill hoses, dryer vent kits, stack kits, dishwasher elbows, anti tip brackets, charcoal filters, trim kits, hood remotes.
Delivery is a row of its own and it names the tier, tailgate, drop off, put in place, delivery into the unit and uncrate, or white glove; vertical transport is a row beside it.
Split install by appliance family rather than one install row: refrigerator, range, cooktop, wall oven, hood, microwave, dishwasher, disposal, washer, dryer, stack kit and panel ready.
The cabinet above the refrigerator, the tall oven cabinet, the drop in stove cabinet and the scribe moulding are casework sized by the appliance; tag them to casework.
Fireplaces, pet grooming dryers and coffee machines carry appliance marks; tag them here and give each a row.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects, not re-mined.
