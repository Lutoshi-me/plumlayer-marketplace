# Drywall

Wood structural framing goes to rough carpentry; drywall boards and finishes those walls, so tag framing and boarding apart.
Non-load-bearing interior partitions at wood framed levels are bid by both trades; tag them to drywall and rough carpentry.
Load-bearing light gauge framing and prefabricated wall panels are their own package; drywall boards and finishes what the system builds.
Blocking is drywall's only inside walls drywall framed; blocking in wood or panel framed walls goes to rough carpentry, and where the framer is unclear tag both.
Welded hollow metal frames install to drywall; a knock-down frame at a stud wall tags drywall and finish carpentry both; doors and hardware to their own package.
Batt and mineral wool ride with drywall; spray, blown and spray applied insulation go to insulation; rigid goes with the envelope unless it sits behind board.
On multifamily tag ceiling tile and its grid to acoustic ceilings and keep the framing, blocking and pockets around it drywall; on other types tag both.
Partition types collapse to wall family rather than a row a mark: metal framed, wood framed, corridor and demising, shaft, exterior backup, furred at existing.
Shaft walls are rows of their own, and their marks enumerate where the assembly changes hands.
Ceilings split detail by detail: draft stops, cove and perimeter lighting framing, garage ceilings, shade and drapery pockets, curved ceilings, channel and clips.
Level of finish splits by condition, not by surface: exposed, concealed, above rated and sound rated walls, tile substrate, wall covering, amenity.
Counted installs each carry a count: hollow metal frames, access panels, fire extinguisher cabinets, corner guards, expansion joint covers, material by others.
Exterior cold formed framing and exterior gypsum sheathing at podium and metal framed levels are drywall; where a panel system carries the sheathing tag both.
Sealant and firestop split by condition: head of wall, base of wall, at windows, at exterior doors, slab edge, acoustical at demising and corridor.
Spray applied fireproofing and its patching go to fireproofing; the head and base sealing drywall does reads alike and is not the same work.
Gypsum floor underlayment and gypcrete are their own package even where a drywall bid carries them.

coverage: derived from a book mined in a fan-out on twenty-five projects, not re-mined.
