# Miscellaneous metals

Aluminum work is a manufacturer's package, not this one: guardrails, balcony rails, prefabricated balconies, fencing, gates and screen walls. Tag them to prefabricated balconies and railings.
Stairs never collapse. One row per stair by number and level range, then five rows under it: stringers treads risers and landings, guardrail, offset handrail at the guardrail, wall mounted handrail, access gate.
Record what each stair is built of. Where the stairs are wood, precast or cast in place, this package is the rails and the nosings only.
Where this trade furnishes and another installs, write two rows and not one. Relieving angles, loose lintels, embeds, prefabricated balconies and aluminum rails all split that way.
Relieving angles, shelf angles and loose lintels route by framing type: structural steel at the steel framed levels, this package at the wood framed levels. The mason installs them either way.
Embeds, embed angles, leveling plates, anchor bolts, shear wall plates and post bases enumerate by condition and not by count, furnish only. The concrete trade sets them.
Stair nosings, abrasive nosings, treads and thresholds are drawn on the stair detail and bought with the floor. Tag them to flooring or specialties.
Wall mounted rails and rail posts need wood blocking that this trade does not carry. Tag the blocking to rough carpentry.
Break elevator metals apart: pit ladder per pit, sill angle per opening, divider beams, hoist and safety beams, sump grates and frames, cants. Hoist beams can sit with the elevator supplier, so tag both.
Split aluminum rail rows by mounting method. Stem mount, fascia mount, surface mount to concrete, mount to pavers and dirt mount are separate rows for one profile.
Enumerate roof metal: catwalk, roof stair, platform and guardrail at low parapet each get a row. Flashing where a post or a leg penetrates the roof goes to roofing.
Glass guardrails are contested. Tag the posts and supports here and the glass panels to glazing, and tag both where a detail shows one assembly.
Read the landscape sheets. Bollards, tree guards, site railings at exterior stairs, fencing, gates, bike racks and catenary posts show there, so tag both this package and landscaping.

coverage: derived from a book written on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
