# Overhead and coiling doors

Elevator smoke curtains are their own package: the same companies sell both and the same document often covers both, so tag a curtain to smoke curtains.
Coiling, rolling, sectional and high speed doors on a door schedule belong here, not to doors, frames and hardware; the swinging doors on that schedule stay theirs.
Door type never collapses: rolling service, insulated rolling, rolling grille, high speed rolling, sectional, fire rated coiling and counter shutter are separate rows at one opening size.
Opening width and height ride the row and split it: two doors of one type at different openings are two rows.
Identical doors at the same size, type, operator and finish are one row with a count; a difference in any of the four splits the row.
The operator and its control scheme are their own row per door: horsepower, phase, control station location and auxiliary chain hoist.
Low voltage control wiring from the operator to the controller and the activation devices is its own row, and the row says how far it runs.
Hood and spring supports, jambs and lintels drawn at a coiling door detail are not this package; tag them to the wall trade at the opening, masonry or structural steel.
Do not route on section number here: coiling doors, sectional doors and smoke curtains have been cited under one another's sections. Route on the product description.
Fire rating rides the row and takes its own row where a rating is called for; write it in minutes.
Safety devices are their own row: sensing edge, photo eyes, monitored safety device, warning light.
Slat gauge, insulation value, cycle rating, windlock type, hood material and bottom bar profile ride the row's description; they are not rows.
What activates a door splits the price. Where a sheet shows no push button, sensor, card reader or loop at an opening, that is a Question, not an assumed push button.
Mount condition and headroom come off the opening detail and ride the row: between jambs or face of wall, and low headroom, which is a product class and not a note.
Fire shutters and accordion or folding fire doors leave this package; a different specialty sells them and they are bid with smoke curtains.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four mid-rise residential projects in one regional market; not re-mined.
