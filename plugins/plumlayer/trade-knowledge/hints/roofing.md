# Roofing

Split every roof assembly mark into layer rows: vapor retarder, base insulation, tapered insulation, coverboard, membrane, overburden.
Roof areas of one assembly collapse into its mark; main roof, elevator and stair overruns and canopies are not their own rows.
Adhesives, fasteners, plates, termination bars, cant strips, cover tape and preformed corners ride the membrane row.
A penetration family is one row with a count, never a row for each: pipe boots, pitch pockets, vent pipes, duct penetrations, drains.
Rooftop objects other trades own get a flash only row each with a count: mechanical curbs, equipment supports, duct supports, screen posts, railing posts, anchor bases, skylights.
Blocking, nailers, sleepers and curbs at the roof are rough carpentry's and roofing flashes them; tag both.
A roof drain takes two rows: the plumber sets and secures the bowl, roofing sets the drain height and installs the clamping ring and basket.
Roofing's metal is what terminates the membrane; a trellis cap or flashing between two runs of siding is siding's, same gauge or not.
Through wall, sill and shelf flashing, reglets and counterflashing are masonry's or siding's; where the wall trade is unclear, tag both.
A terrace assembly splits at the top of the membrane, so give the membrane below and the protection sheet and pavers above separate rows.
A deck of hot fluid applied rubberized asphalt over structural slab is waterproofing whole, drainage board and rigid insulation with it.
A rooftop equipment screen takes four rows on four packages: frame and panels, base blocking, cladding by face, and post flashing with a count.
Roof anchors, davits and permanent fall protection are their own package, and roofing gets a flash only row with a count.
A hatch safety rail kit comes with the hatch and is roofing's; a permanent fixed or ballasted guard rail is misc metals'.
Name the performance value on the row and not just the layer: membrane mils, coverboard thickness, metal gauge, wind uplift rating.
R-value takes two rows, minimum and average, because sets state both.
The vapor barrier takes its own row with its grade, and its lap onto the wall barrier at a parapet is a Question, not a row on either trade.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects, three new multifamily or mixed use and one renovation; not re-mined.
