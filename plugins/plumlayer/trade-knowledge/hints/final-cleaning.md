# Final cleaning

Pass is the first axis: a rough clean, a detail clean and a touch-up after the punch list are three rows, not one cleaning row.
Then area: units, corridors, stairs, lobbies and mail rooms, amenity, back of house and mechanical rooms, basement, garage, retail, roof and terraces, and any sub-basement each get a row.
Unit by unit enumeration collapses to one units row with a count, and the operations inside a room type collapse to that room type.
Interior glass, the interior face of exterior windows with their mullions and sills, and the exterior face are three rows; a single windows row is always ambiguous.
Exterior glass names its access method on the row, and the access itself is a second row; where no sheet fixes the method, ask.
Garage cleaning names its method, sweeping or power washing; sheets do not fix it, so ask rather than writing a bare garage row.
Protective film on exterior windows sits between final cleaning and glazing; tag both where the sheet does not say who pulls it.
Split protection removal by what is protected, floor, wall and countertop; it is contested, so tag final cleaning with flooring or countertops.
Appliance shipping material and protective film removal is final cleaning; appliance assembly can sit either side, so tag both.
Elevator cab, door, floor and frame cleaning is final cleaning, not the elevator package.
Overspray, caulk, grout and adhesive removal is its own row and goes back to the trade that left it, painting or drywall.
Roof and terrace washing is its own row, kept out of the areas row.
Phasing and remobilization is its own row with a count: a corridor cleaned once per phase is cleaned that many times.
Carry one row for hourly or crew day work beyond the signed scope, and one for supplies, equipment, supervision and insurance.
Turnover cleaning stays on final cleaning even where the demolition package carries debris removal.
Trash rooms, interior signage, bike rooms and bike racks each get their own row before turnover.

coverage: derived from a book written on four multifamily projects, drafted from those four rather than mined in a fan-out; not re-mined.
