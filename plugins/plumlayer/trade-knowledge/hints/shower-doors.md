# Shower Doors and Enclosures

Count doors and surrounds as separate rows with separate counts; they are never the same number, because tubs and roll-in showers take a surround and no hinged door.
Read the finish schedule for the surround material first: a cast marble or molded surround is this package and tiling stops at the bathroom floor; where the surround is tile, only the door is this package and the surround is tiling's.
Tag shower enclosures and doors here where the project carries this package and to specialties where it does not, even where the finish schedule mark reads as a glazing mark.
Enumerate by fixture type with a count each: standard shower at each drawn size, roll-in or accessible shower, and tub; individual bathrooms collapse into that count.
A surround can be priced as a back panel plus two side panels, so a panel count can run about three times the room count; keep panel counts on their own rows and never compare one against a room count.
Pan count and pan type are their own rows with the drain type named; centre drain, trench drain and roll-in pans are separate products at separate rates.
The pan is supplied on this package and set by plumbing; tag the pan here and its setting and drain connection to plumbing.
Split supply from install: install is its own row with the counterparty named, and it has been carried by the supplier, by a certified installer and inside the finish carpentry package. Never write one shower enclosures line.
Enclosure configuration is its own row: door only, door and panel, or panel door panel, with or without a header and with the channel condition named.
Accessories each take their own row and their own count: corner soap dish, foot rest, accessory ledge and trim sticks; their counts differ from the room count and from each other.
At a roll-in or accessible shower, carry the clear opening the enclosure leaves as an attribute on its row; it is the dimension compliance turns on.
Hardware finish and pull style is its own row; where the set calls out more than one finish for the same scope, raise a Question rather than picking one.

coverage: derived from a book written on four mid-rise residential projects in one market, none with a tiled shower and none addressing waterproofing; not re-mined.
