# HVAC

Equipment: one row per schedule mark family with its count, never a row per unit; take the tag vocabulary off this engineer's schedules, not a standard prefix list.
Duct: rows by material and service, galvanized rectangular, galvanized round, flexible, ductboard, rated assemblies and lining, never a measured length; fittings, boots, hangers, sleeves, gauge and seam type ride the row.
Dampers: a row per kind, radiation, fire, fire smoke, motorized, volume, backdraft and corridor balancing, even where the plan repeats one symbol.
Access panels: rows by location and by whether rated, at apartments, at fire and smoke dampers, at exhaust fans, at motorized dampers and at ceiling equipment; keeping a motorized damper in a unit puts a panel at each one.
Line voltage, power wiring, final connections and loose disconnects tag to electrical; low voltage control wiring to devices is this package and the conduit carrying it is electrical's.
Condensate: this package runs condensate to an open drain and the riser is plumbing's; keep condensate rows, drain pans and safety switches separate from refrigerant piping.
Roof support is a block of rows, curbs, rails, stands, sleepers, blocking, vibration isolation, flashing and counterflashing; tag flashing and counterflashing to both this package and roofing.
Dryer exhaust: the duct run, the wall box and the exterior cap are this package, about one run per dwelling unit; the dryer and its vent kit ride with appliances.
Registers, grilles and diffusers: one family row with a count, not a row per mark, and collapse room by room duct routing to the space type and the system.
Controls take three rows: thermostats and local controls, the manufacturer's own system controller and gateway, and the building automation system, which is usually not this package.
Wall caps, dryer caps and hood ducts are often drawn only on architectural sheets; tag them to this package where you see them there.
Ventilation architecture is its own row, central rooftop outside air with common risers or an energy recovery ventilator per apartment, and not just an equipment count.
Kitchen hoods: the hood rides with appliances and the duct from it out to the exterior wall is this package.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market for one general contractor; no hydronic, boiler or gas fired work; not re-mined.
