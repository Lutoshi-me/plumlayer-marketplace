# Siding

Everything outboard of the sheathing is siding: continuous insulation, girts or clips, hat channel or furring, cavity insect screen.
Every cladding row carries a finish state: prefinished, primed for field finish, or unfinished. Field painting of cladding and trim tags to painting; a primed mark needs a painting row beside it.
Wall assembly is the axis, not elevation. Each assembly tag carrying cladding takes a row beside the mark; the same mark over four backups is four rows.
One row per cladding mark on the exterior finish schedule; profile, exposure, thickness, fastening, color and accessories ride it; elevations, levels and piece counts collapse in.
The air and vapor barrier lands in siding on some jobs and framing on others, so tag both; panelized walls take it in the shop with the framing.
Girts take a row with their depth and a second for thermally broken or not; insulation a row with its thickness. Where specification and drawings disagree, ask.
Copings, parapet caps, gravel stops and break metal at top of wall tag to roofing, except a coping in the cladding's own composite material, which is siding's.
One row per flashing condition: base, head, jamb, sill, top of wall, corners, end wall, thru-wall, masonry and setback roof transitions, balcony ledgers, over trim boards.
Sheathing, framing, blocking and nailers tag to rough carpentry and light gauge to drywall, blocking outboard of the barrier included; exterior gypsum sheathing to drywall.
Thin brick, masonry, precast and granite tag to masonry, and so do the furring and insulation behind brick and the flashing, lintels and angles at brick.
Split sealant and backer rod five ways: within the cladding system, at its own window and door openings, to masonry, to dissimilar materials, at penetrations.
Split trim by condition: base wall, eave and rake, head and jamb at openings, horizontal bands, water table, corners; reveals and joint trims their own row by profile.
One row per soffit condition: canopy, terrace, balcony undersides, exterior ceilings; undersides and terrace soffits sit on the roofing and balcony boundary, tag both.
Windows, doors and storefronts are their own package; the install tags rough carpentry and siding, the window flange tie-in both.

coverage: derived from a book written on four multifamily projects in one market, all bid by one general contractor; not re-mined.
