# Flooring

Split by area first: units, corridors, amenity and front of house, back of house, stairs, garage, lobby and vestibule, retail. One row per finish mark inside each area.
A mark range is one row only where the whole range shares one product and one installation.
Rooms collapse to area plus mark; a residential room never gets its own row, though a named amenity room does where its mark changes.
Levels collapse to a range unless the finish or the substrate changes; where concrete meets gypcrete at a level line, both sides carry their own rows.
Resilient, sculpted profile and rubber base tag to flooring; primed wood and MDF base to finish carpentry and painting, and shop finished base to millwork.
Base splits from flooring, one row per base mark, and by area: base inside units and base in common areas are answered apart.
Transitions and thresholds are their own group, split at least into unit entry thresholds, interior transitions and reducers, and tile edge trim.
Wall tile, tub and shower surrounds and backsplash tag to flooring wherever tile sits in the package, each its own row with its stated height.
Sealed or densified concrete and resinous or epoxy floor marks take their own row and tag both flooring and painting.
Gypsum underlayment and gypcrete on an assembly detail tag to their own package, never to flooring.
Sound underlayment belongs to no package by default: tag the mat under a poured topping to gypsum underlayment and under tile to tiling, split it by area, and never ride it on a flooring row.
Adhesive, thinset, grout, caulk, patching compound, weld rod and fasteners ride the row they belong to; none of them is a row.
Colour, thickness, wear layer and installation method ride the mark's row and are written on it.
Unit counts collapse to a unit type row or a whole units row; the unit count is the denominator, not a row.
Waterproofing and crack suppression membrane split into floor and wall, and split again by where the schedule applies them.
Stair treads, risers, nosings, landings, tactile warning strips and stringer material each take their own row and tag to flooring.
Elevator cab flooring is flooring's, never elevators'; solid surface, stone slab, pedestal pavers, fireplace surrounds and acoustical wall panels are not flooring.

coverage: derived from a book written on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
