# Countertops

Counters are their own package even where a detail shows them sitting on cabinets. Tag the top to countertops and the cabinet, its end panels and its shop drawings to casework.
Sink cutouts and faucet holes are countertops whoever supplies the sink, and the faucet holes ride the cutout row.
Sink supply and sink setting split both ways. Write two rows and tag both countertops and plumbing. Faucets, drains, disposals and connections are plumbing.
Kitchen backsplash is tile. The four inch back and side splash at vanities is countertops. Split splash rows by room and again by height.
Unit tops collapse to plan type, or to the group of plan types with the same counter geometry. Never a row per apartment and never a row per counter run.
Vanity tops enumerate by width, not by plan type, and plans with two baths carry two, so vanity count runs above unit count.
Amenity tops enumerate one row per room keyed to that room's mark. Two counters in one room in one mark with one edge are one row.
Carry the material mark and its thickness on every row. Thickness sets the rate and decides whether a substrate is needed.
Split edge wherever the profile changes. A mitered apron carries its face dimension and measures by length, and appliance edges split from finished edges.
Split sink cutouts by type and opening size. Undermount kitchen, undermount vanity and drop in are different rows, and a two sink vanity is another.
Cooktop cutout is its own row and lands on the barrier free plan types, where a freestanding range also means no splash behind it.
Stone on a cabinet face or side, and stone cladding on a floating shelf, is countertops. The structure it hangs on is millwork. Tag both.
Waterfall legs and double miters get their own rows with face and return dimensions. The stone wrap is countertops and a finished end panel beside it is casework.
Thresholds and saddles are tile even where they carry stone marks off the same finish schedule.
Cast and cultured marble bath products, shower bases and tub and shower surrounds are not countertops even where the same maker supplies vanity tops.
Unit tops are not only kitchens and baths. Laundry, bedroom and desk tops appear and each is its own row.

coverage: derived from a book mined on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined. Nothing here is tested against commercial or renovation work.
