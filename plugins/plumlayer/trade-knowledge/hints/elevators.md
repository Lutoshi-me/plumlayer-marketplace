# Elevators

Two grain regimes: new construction rows configure equipment per car, modernization rows mark each existing component new or retained; never mix them on one car.
Every car is a row by its mark, and per car a row each for capacity, speed, travel, stop count, and front and rear openings counted separately.
Stop count is what every bid is compared on; where the sheet leaves it ambiguous raise a Question rather than pick a number.
Product class per car is its own row, not an attribute: it sets pit depth, overhead, beam ratings and whether a machine room exists.
The hoistway envelope per car is rows, not notes: clear width and depth, pit depth, clear overhead, hoist beam and lifeline beam ratings.
Hall and car fixtures, buttons, lanterns and indicators collapse to one fixtures row per car.
Cab interior finishes collapse to one finish package row per car carrying the flooring recess depth and weight limit; entrance finishes are their own row per car.
Landings collapse to a stop count plus a front and rear split, unless a car changes opening sides partway up the rise, which is its own line.
Hoist beams and lifeline beams in the hoistway are structural steel; where the sheet shows one furnished by the elevator contractor, tag both.
Rail bracket supports, intermediate supports and separator beams are structural steel or masonry; the inserts are usually elevators, so tag both.
Machine beams are elevators; their supports, the machine room slab and its blockouts and pockets are concrete and structural steel.
Pit lighting, receptacles, disconnects, feeders, shunt trip and the dedicated phone or data line per car are electrical, not elevators.
Pit waterproofing is waterproofing and the sump, pump, cover and grating are plumbing, even where the elevator pit section draws them.
Cab flooring material is flooring, never elevators; the elevator cab row carries the recess depth and weight limit that bind it.
Where hoistway entrances and frames are shown prime finished rather than factory finished, painting carries the finish, not elevators.
Hoistway venting and machine room ventilation, heating and cooling are hvac, not elevators.
Trash and linen chutes share division 14 with elevators and share nothing else; tag them to trash chutes.

coverage: derived from a book written on four projects, all bid by one general contractor; not re-mined.
