# Prefabricated balconies

Terrace, patio, courtyard, amenity deck and roof walkway railing usually rides this package on the same extrusion; tag it here and name the run's location on the row.
The deck and its railing are one product on one order, so tag balcony railing here rather than to misc metals, however separately the rail is set.
Ballasted non penetrating roof guardrail is its own package; tag it to misc metals, and say on the row which package the roof egress run belongs to.
Blocking, backing and the framing that receive knife plates and post bases go to rough carpentry, or to structural steel where the support is steel; where the framer is unclear tag both.
Waterproofing and flashing at every balcony attachment and every railing post that penetrates a deck, a paver system or a parapet go to waterproofing, or to roofing on a roof.
Balcony doors are bought in the window package; tag the door to windows and the deck, its railing and the attachment here.
Knife plates, tie rods, clevises, brackets and post bases are supplied here as steel inside an aluminum order, take a row of their own keyed to the structural detail, and are set by whoever installs.
Write the attachment method on every railing row: face mount to studs or blocking, stem mount, fascia mount, top mount, mount to pavers, side mount to a concrete parapet. Each sits on a different trade's substrate.
Railing splits on three axes at once, location, attachment method and infill, so railing takes more rows than balconies even though it is the smaller quantity.
A balcony type is set by its size and by how the deck is hung, and the knife plate count follows from the hanging; those three ride one row.
Supply and install are separate buys here; split install from supply, and split install again between balconies and railings.
Gates split out of the run they interrupt, one row each, with their hardware.
Glass infill railing can be the same extrusion with a glass panel or a glazing buy; tag both.
Privacy screens, fencing and pergola or cabana frames can sit here or with landscaping; tag both, one row each.
Engineering, the stamped submittal and the shop drawings take one row.

coverage: derived from a book written on two mid-rise residential projects in one market; not re-mined. Every balcony in it is aluminum, so nothing here is tested against steel, precast or composite balconies.
