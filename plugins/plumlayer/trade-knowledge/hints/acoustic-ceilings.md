# Acoustic ceilings

Suspended tile on grid is this package. Gypsum board ceilings, soffits and bulkheads are drywall wherever they show on a reflected ceiling plan.
The mark is the axis, not the room. One row per ceiling mark in the finish schedule with its location list, and floors collapse into that list unless the ceiling type changes by floor.
Tile, grid, hangers and standard edge molding inside one mark collapse to that mark's row.
Attributes do not ride the mark row here. Tile manufacturer and item number, panel size, edge condition, and grid manufacturer, profile width and series each get their own checkable row under the mark.
Perimeter and transition trim is its own row in linear feet, apart from standard edge molding, and the drawings often do not show it.
Openings, cutouts and trim rings in the ceiling field are this package. The devices that go in them are electrical, mechanical and fire protection.
A ceiling drawn on a reflected ceiling plan with no mark is real and is not small. Give it its own row and ask what it is.
Above-ceiling insulation is not this package. Tag it to insulation.
Suspension is its own row naming grid duty rating, bracing and seismic restraint.
Ceiling access panels are their own row with a count off the reflected ceiling plans.
Acoustical sealant at edge moldings is its own row.
Finished ceilings at garages, parking, loading docks, trash rooms and closets each get their own row.
Monolithic acoustical gypsum board looks like a tile system and installs like plaster. Ceilings and drywall both take it, so tag both and ask.
Felt, textile and baffle panels come to this package off the finish schedule, on ceilings and on walls. Some marks go to stretch wall installers, so tag both where the panel construction is unclear.
Each felt, textile or baffle mark is its own row with its own area, never sharing a row with tile.
Where an assembly type reads as either suspended acoustical tile or two layers of gypsum board, tag both and ask which it is.
Where the finish schedule names a tile and a variant of the same tile, record the mark and ask which is wanted; never pick one.
Below about forty units, enumerate rooms rather than marks; ceilings there run a few dozen square feet each.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one regional market; not re-mined.
