# Insulation

Cavity batt, sound attenuation blanket and air sealing before the board goes up can all sit in the drywall package; tag insulation and tag drywall as a candidate.
Air sealing after the board is up is not insulation's; tag it to drywall.
Cavity work and sprayed or applied work take different grain: cavity rows follow product and framed assembly, sprayed rows follow the assembly and the location covered.
Group marks by product and thickness, one row each, and carry mark names, R-value, facing and layer count as attributes; two layers filling one cavity is one row.
Split thermal from acoustic even on adjacent walls in one area, keep the walls inside the units their own row, and raise a Question where those walls carry no callout.
Split exterior wall cavity rows where the framing changes: wood stud, metal stud and masonry back-up take different products at the same R-value.
Board insulation outside the framed cavity is not this package: at the cladding backup it is siding's, under slabs and at foundation walls it is concrete's, above the roof deck it is roofing's.
Give the coating over exposed spray foam its own row naming the assembly it covers; it never rides inside the foam row.
The garage or plenum ceiling under conditioned space gets its own row with the product family named; the sheets rarely settle which product answers it.
Rim, band joist and box sill get their own row, apart from the walls above and the floors below.
Parapet and stem wall cavities get a row with the material named.
Keep the small foam locations as one named group listing their details: roof drains, penetrations, anchors, sleeper curbs, dunnage flashing.
Low expansion foam at window and door perimeters is its own row; the caulk at those jambs is other work.
The air barrier over the sheathing is its own package; where closed cell foam is the barrier it is also the insulation, so tag both.
Pipe, duct and equipment insulation and duct sealing are mechanical work, not this package; tag them there.
Firestopping at rated assemblies is its own package, not this trade's; where bought with the trades it goes to the penetrating trade.
Thermal break pads at balcony, canopy and shelf angle connections come off the structural sheets to miscellaneous metals, counted per connection point.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
