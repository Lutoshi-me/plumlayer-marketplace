# Finish carpentry

Every row says whether this trade furnishes the thing or only installs it; it installs doors, casework and specialties others furnish.
Doors, frames and hardware: tag the furnish to that package and the install here.
Split doors into leaf install, frame install and hardware install, then split frames by wall type and leaves by unit interior, unit entry, common, stair and exterior.
Welded hollow metal frames go to drywall, frames at masonry or concrete to masonry; knock-down frames at stud walls tag here and drywall both.
Unit kitchen and bath casework: tag the furnish to casework and the install here, and give the removable sink base and cooktop cabinets their own rows.
Enumerate trim by family and location: unit, bathroom, corridor and amenity base, window stool, apron and extension jamb, entry and interior door casing, cased opening jamb and casing, stair skirt and wall cap.
Every base and millwork mark on the finish schedule gets a row; species, profile, thickness and finish ride the row as attributes.
Vinyl and rubber base goes to flooring; where the schedule makes the unit and corridor base vinyl, almost nothing is left for this trade to furnish.
Closet shelving splits by closet type and tier count, and the material is a row of its own; wire, melamine and MDF are three numbers for one geometry.
Blocking and backing behind shelving, grab bars, accessories, mailboxes and wall hung casework is set before drywall closes; tag it to rough carpentry.
Bath accessories, medicine cabinets, mirrors, shower rods, grab bars, fire extinguisher cabinets and mailboxes are installed here, furnished elsewhere, each family its own row.
Window jambs fork: a drywall return leaves no extension jamb or casing here, a wood jamb puts both here; where the detail does not say, tag drywall and finish carpentry.
Setting microwaves, cooktops and wall ovens sits here; ranges, stacking kits and anti-tip brackets go to appliances.
Wood framed stairs bring plywood treads, risers, skirt boards and wall caps; tag those to finish carpentry and rough carpentry, and the metal handrails and pan stairs to misc metals.
Amenity and common area millwork enumerates room by room and by interior design detail number; tag it to finish carpentry and millwork, since the boundary moves.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
