# Window treatments

Ceiling pockets, soffits and recesses that conceal a shade are drywall's; tag the pocket there and the shade here.
Blocking and backing for shade brackets is not this package: tag it to rough carpentry or to drywall, and tag both where the framer is unclear.
Motorized shades: the shade and its motor are this package; motor power, control wiring, low voltage runs and any building system interface are electrical. Tag both.
Split shades by finish schedule mark where the schedule marks them, then by location; individual windows collapse into a count per mark per location.
Apartment units, amenity and common areas, leasing and office, fitness and any specialty room each carry their own count row and never collapse together.
Top treatment carries its own row, never an attribute: fascia, exposed roll, pocket or cassette. Where the set shows no head detail, write the row and ask.
On the count row say what is in it: balcony and terrace door leaves, common area and lobby glass, attic stock, and whether a wide window takes one shade or two.
Balcony and terrace doors carry their own row, and common area and lobby glass another; neither folds into the unit shade count.
Drapery carries its own rows, never inside shade rows, measured in fabric yards with fabrication and install separate; whether it sits in this package or the owner's furniture package is a Question.
Attic stock carries its own row with a stated quantity or a stated zero.
Control type carries its own row: manual clutch, cordless, motorized.
Amenity treatments are drawn in the interior design package rather than the architectural set; treatments on those sheets are this package's.
A valance or a millwork recess a shade sits in is finish carpentry's; the shade in it is this package's.
Jamb depth and the head condition an inside-mount shade needs belong to the window package; tag the opening there and the treatment here.
Carry the specified manufacturer, fabric and openness percentage onto the row: the specified product is often unavailable at the drawn sizes or barred by local fabric rules, so a substitution has to be legible.
Levels collapse into a level range unless the product changes.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four mid-rise residential projects in one market, apartment shades plus a small amenity scope; not re-mined.
