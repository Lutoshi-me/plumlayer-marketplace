# Electrical

Where a bidder could carry labor and not material, the row says furnish and install: fixtures, service gear, generator, security and network equipment, appliances, mechanical equipment.
Fire alarm, tel and data, access control, camera and intercom sheets are electrical work by default; tag electrical and low voltage both.
Receptacles, switches, boxes, circuits and homeruns collapse to one devices row per area type: units, corridors, amenity, back of house, garage, site.
Conduit, fittings, supports, hangers and sleeves collapse into the work they serve, never a row of their own.
Panel by panel and circuit by circuit collapse into one distribution row. An incomplete panel schedule is a Question, not a reason to enumerate.
Split lighting three ways: fixtures, controls, and the install of either. Decorative and architectural fixtures take their own row.
Conductor material is its own row. Where the feeder schedule shows both aluminum and copper, the row is not optional.
Electric strikes, mag locks, power transfers, request to exit devices and unit locks go to doors frames and hardware; the box, flex and cable back are electrical.
Power wiring and disconnects to mechanical and plumbing equipment are electrical; starters, drives, thermostats and control wiring of any voltage go with the equipment.
Read the equipment schedule note: where a disconnect arrives with the unit, electrical carries neither its furnish nor its install.
Electrical power wires the appliances; cords, pigtails and anti tip devices go with the appliance package. Accessible wall ovens are hardwired where the rest are cord and plug.
Pads, pole bases, duct bank encasement and the curb at busway penetrations are concrete work; trenching and backfill for electrical conduit are earthwork.
Split the utility line by party: primary conduits, primary conductors, manholes, vaults, transformers, pads, transclosures, core holes and perimeter ground bussing.
The generator splits: the set, its enclosure, the pad or curb, transfer switches, the fuel or day tank, the rigging into position and the feeders.
Give each life safety subsystem its own row: smoke control, stair pressurization, voice evacuation, area of rescue and elevator two way communication.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
