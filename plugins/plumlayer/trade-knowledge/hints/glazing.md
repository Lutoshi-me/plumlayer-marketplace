# Glazing

Aluminum entrances and storefront doors carry their own frames and hardware sets; tag them to glazing, not to the door package.
Glass in a hollow metal door or frame is glazing; the door and the frame are not. Split the lites by frame type and rating.
Fire rated glazed openings are often called out only on the door schedule. Read it for them and tag them to glazing.
Shower enclosures tag to shower doors where the project carries that package, to specialties where it does not; split by configuration.
Count enclosures and surrounds separately. Not every shower that gets a surround gets a glass door.
Split every mirror mark: unit bathroom, amenity, fitness, elevator; tag each to glazing and specialties, illuminated ones to electrical too.
Blocking and plywood behind mirrors, railings and enclosures is never glazing. Tag it to rough carpentry.
Carry furnish and install mode on every work family row. Glaze only and framing furnished are different scopes at one opening.
Split storefront marks exterior from interior. Do not split the lites inside a mark; the mark is the row.
Give every entrance door its own row by door number with its hardware set number.
A hardware set is one row at its opening. Do not split hinges, closers, exit devices or thresholds out of it.
The storefront perimeter joint is its own row, split exterior bead from interior bead; painters do not carry aluminum to drywall.
Give the barrier and the membrane flashing behind the perimeter joint their own row; tag them to waterproofing.
Glass railing and guardrail glass, base shoe and gaskets are glazing. The cap is its own row, to carpentry or metals.
Glazing supplies and installs automatic operators and actuators. Wiring, power supplies and card readers are electrical.
Louvers drawn on the storefront schedule are not glazing. Tag them to mechanical.
Where the set has no separate window package, tag aluminum windows to glazing; where it has one, tag both.
Applied film is glazing and lands on no other package. Split every film mark; elevator door film counts by the leaf.
Smoke baffles and draft curtains at open stairs are glazing, measured in linear feet of tempered laminated glass.
Wood or veneered frames at glass partitions go to millwork; glazing carries the glass, doors and door hardware.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
