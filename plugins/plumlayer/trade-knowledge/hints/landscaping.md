# Landscaping

Split every area on grade from over structure and keep the rows apart; the build-up, the access and the trade on the other side change across that line.
Area is the top axis: every terrace, courtyard, roof level and at-grade zone carries its own rows, named as the drawings name them.
Where a materials legend exists it is the spine: its mark families are the row families and its mark keys the row names, and the mark letters are this architect's own.
Give every paving mark its own row; precast, permeable, porcelain, wood tile, vehicular and stepping stone are separate products, never one unit paving row.
At grade split each paving mark build-up layer by layer: setting bed, concrete base, aggregate base, geotextile, pedestals, edging; one pavers row leaves the stack unassigned.
Over structure the green roof stack is one assembly row rather than a row a layer; the insulation over the membrane detaches and takes its own row.
The assembly above the waterproofing membrane is landscaping; the membrane, its flashing and its penetrations go to waterproofing, and pavers on pedestals take both tags.
Concrete footings under everything the landscaper sets, bollards, benches, pergolas, cabanas and fences, go to concrete; bollard wiring and controls to electrical, the fixture stays here.
Irrigation is a landscaping row; the point of connection, backflow and meter go to plumbing, the wiring, conduit and coring to electrical.
Soil splits by depth and by location, not by area: lawn, bed, planter and tree pit at one depth are four rows, and structural soil is a row of its own.
Plants split by species, size and count, never by individual plant: trees, shrubs, perennials and grasses, groundcover and annuals, with trees again by size band.
Read the legend notes column first: a mark noted see civil or see structural leaves this package, and retaining walls, site walls and site paving route away there.
Railings, guardrails, tree guards and tree grates draw on the landscape sheets and go to misc metals.
Bike racks, trash receptacles and benches drift between this package and specialties; tag both where a sheet shows them.
Fire features and outdoor kitchen goods are landscaping to buy and set; their gas runs and final connections go to plumbing, their wiring to electrical.

coverage: derived from a book written on three multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
