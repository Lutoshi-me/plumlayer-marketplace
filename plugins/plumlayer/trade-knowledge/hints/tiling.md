# Tiling

Wet areas split by element, not by mark: shower wall, shower floor, pan, curb, niche, ceiling, tub surround, tub face, bath floor, bath base and backsplash each take a row.
Waterproofing and crack isolation membrane at wet areas are the tile setter's to install; split floor from wall as their own rows, never an attribute of a tile row.
The finish schedule mixes packages: carpet, resilient plank, wood, rubber tread and riser, vinyl and rubber base and entrance mat are flooring; tile and stone marks are tiling.
Enumerate every finish mark on the schedule, trim tile marks included, even where the drawings group them.
Tile extent is rows of its own: courses above the shower floor and the tub deck, tile to the ceiling, tub face tiled, backsplash height, tile behind vanities, where tile stops.
Cement backer board and tile underlayment: most tile setters exclude it, so it sits with the wall it goes on. Tag it both tiling and drywall.
Acoustic underlayment mat under bath and corridor floors is specified in another section and is the tile setter's more often than not; put thickness and product on the row.
Shower drains are the plumber's and tile setters exclude them; tag the drain to plumbing and the tiled pan to tiling.
Thresholds and saddles are tiling, counted each per opening and split by location: unit entry, bathroom entry, roll-in shower.
Metal trims and transition profiles are tiling, one row per profile and finish off the transition detail; never count pieces.
A pitched mud bed and site built pan is a different row from a prefabricated pan; a job can use one at standard units and the other at accessible units.
Grout splits by type and by area: epoxy or water cleanable grout at lobby and public floors takes its own row from the standard grout.
Counters, solid surface and window sills are countertops; the backsplash above the counter is tiling, so the joint takes a row.
Movement and control joints take their own row; a sheet silent on them is a gap, not an inclusion.
Shower doors and shower glass are never tiling; they tag to shower doors where the project carries that package and to specialties where it does not.
Where the set shows cast marble panels and pans, the unit shower and tub tile leaves tiling; the kitchen backsplash stays tiling.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
