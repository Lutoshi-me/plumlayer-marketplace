# Trash chutes

Each chute is its own row, keyed to its trash room, with diameter, floors over intakes, and the rough floor opening it needs. Never enumerate a chute by the storey.
Chute body, sections, intake throats and retainer flanges collapse into the chute row. Intake doors are their own row with size, hinge, label rating, finish, handle and keying.
Compactors, compaction and non-compaction containers and wheeled toters are chute work even where the specification puts them elsewhere; the two container kinds are separate rows.
The chute shaft, its fire rating, its fire wrap and its fire caulking are never chutes. Tag the enclosure to drywall and the sealing to whoever firestops it.
Floor and roof openings the chute passes through, and their cutting and framing, are never chutes. Tag slab openings to concrete.
All interlock and compactor wiring, the control box connections and the power feed are electrical, a low voltage interlock included. Only the devices are chutes.
Sprinkler heads set in the chute are chutes, furnish only; the piping to them and its testing are fire protection. Name the floors the heads occur on.
The wash down unit, its solution, its access door and its solenoid are chutes, furnish only; hot water, shutoff, backflow preventer and the floor drain are plumbing.
The roof curb, its framing, its flashing and the roofing around it are not chutes even where the drawings call for one; tag it to roofing. Venting is a chute row.
Standard floor supports at each level are chutes. Added support inside the shaft, its blocking and the frame it sits on are excluded by chutes; tag misc metals and rough carpentry both.
A heat sensor in the chute is chutes. The smoke detector device beside it is not and never rides a chute row.
Sound treatment is two rows, the sprayed coating with its grade and the isolation pads with their type and height. Pad height sets intake door height, so carry it.
The discharge assembly is its own row naming the door type and whether it releases on a fusible link or on a solenoid. The two are not interchangeable.
The interlock system is its own row naming voltage class, whether a control panel is required and what it ties into. An interlocked chute carries a manual on and off switch.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
