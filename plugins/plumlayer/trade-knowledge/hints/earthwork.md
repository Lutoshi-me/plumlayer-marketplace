# Earthwork and Sitework

Site utilities outside the building are earthwork: sewer, water, drain, gas, electric and telecom, not plumbing, electrical or low voltage.
Under-slab excavation, bedding and backfill are earthwork; the pipe and conduit are the trade's own, and where the plumber claims the footprint tag both.
Earthwork excavates, forms and encases the duct bank; conduit, spacers, pull strings, sweeps and grounding inside it go to electrical or low voltage.
Cut and cap of existing services at the street is earthwork; demolition draws its line at the building footprint and takes the slab and foundation removal inside it.
Cast in place site concrete is not earthwork: walks, ramps, pads, mud mats and the slab under a detention system. Tag the base under them here.
Earthwork strips and stockpiles or exports topsoil; importing, screening, amending and placing loam, seeding and irrigation go to landscaping.
Foundation waterproofing, insulation, vapor barrier, drainage mat and protection board go to waterproofing; earthwork's rows are the excavation and the backfill.
Sheeting, shoring, underpinning and aggregate piers leave this package; the pre-trench, working pad, spoils and stone load transfer pad earthwork does for them are rows here.
Two regimes under one name: on an open site the balance carries the rows; on a constrained urban site most rows are street work and seams.
Never one cut and fill line: split the balance by movement, strip and export topsoil, strip and stockpile, cut and export unsuitable, cut and stockpile for reuse, fill to subgrade, import structural fill.
Split export by disposal classification tier, a row a tier; split import by material and destination, structural fill, base under slabs, base under pavement, sand bedding, loam.
Foundation excavation collapses to perimeter, interior, courtyard, garage and pits, never a row a room or a grid line.
Count drainage and sewer structures by type: manholes by diameter, catch basins with and without sump, area drains, water quality units, trench drain by length.
Each cut and cap and each new service into the building is its own row, by service, with the street named and the point the service stops stated.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects in one market, three urban sites and one open greenfield; not re-mined.
