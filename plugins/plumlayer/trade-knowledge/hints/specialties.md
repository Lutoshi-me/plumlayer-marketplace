# Specialties

Give every work family a furnish row and an install row: the same product furnished and the same product installed are two scopes.
Accessory schedules enumerate here: every mark on the schedule is its own row with its count, and the mark vocabulary is whatever that schedule uses.
Illuminated mirrors: their own row, split from plain mirrors, and tag glazing and electrical alongside specialties.
Fire extinguisher cabinets: trimless recessed ones are set by drywall and surface and semi recessed ones are not, so tag the install by cabinet type.
Shower doors, surrounds and pans are three populations with three counts; doors tag to shower doors where the project carries that package and here where it does not.
Mailboxes: the quantities are tenant doors, parcel doors and outgoing slots, not mailbox units.
Signage is its own package with its own bidders; keep signs off the specialties rows entirely.
Wire and closet shelving: count shelves, and tag finish carpentry alongside specialties.
Bike racks are drawn in Division 12 and belong here; count bike spaces by rack type, not racks.
Wall protection is three products under one phrase: rigid corner guards stay here, sheet wall protection and wall covering do not, and impact rated panels have gone to painting.
Wire mesh tenant storage: locker count, tier configuration, ceilings and locks are four separate rows.
Medicine cabinets: their own row, not mirrors, and a recessed one needs an opening no package claims, so tag drywall or rough carpentry with it.
Extinguishers and cabinets are separate rows, rated cabinets separate from unrated; the rating sits on the life safety sheets, not the accessory schedule.
Pet spa and dog wash: equipment rows here, the tub to plumbing, the connections to plumbing and electrical.
Window treatments and trash chutes are their own packages; keep both off the specialties rows.
Fireplaces are not specialties even though the amenity equipment around them is; keep hearth work off these rows.
Amenity and common area accessory marks are a separate mark set from the unit marks and get their own rows.
Expansion joint cover assemblies: measure them in ten foot lengths, and give them a row wherever the set shows them.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined. Toilet partitions do not appear at all.
