# Support of excavation

The set shows an excavation, not a retention system; measure the run and the depth and write one wall row whose method and extent the bidder states.
All excavation is earthwork, including digging past the pile face so lagging sets flush and the trenches and heel blocks for rakers; tag it earthwork.
Dewatering, survey and layout at the pile line, and the working bench and tracked rig access along the wall are earthwork, not this package.
Piles do not enumerate a row a mark; the count comes off the wall length and the spacing, so keep them in the wall row and carry length in plan view.
Exposed face is derived, not measured off a sheet; write it with the cut depth it was taken on, as a maximum and as a range, and say which depth was used.
Lagging species and treatment ride the wall row as attributes, and drilling, grouting, casing and their spoils stay inside it rather than becoming rows.
Tag walers, corner braces and rakers here, and write on the row that they stand in the hole while the concrete package forms and pours the foundation walls.
Split internal bracing into setting and removal; removal is a second visit and a bidder can carry one and not the other.
Where a section shows a foundation wall poured against the retention wall, tag both this package and concrete and write the one sided form condition on the row.
A membrane run against the retention wall is waterproofing; tag the wall here and the membrane and its form tie detail to waterproofing.
Underpinning at an abutting structure is its own block of rows, length, height, depth and volume, kept apart from the wall even where the same bidder does both.
Where the excavation runs against an abutting existing wall, tag a retention row and an underpinning row both; whether that wall serves instead is the bidder's answer.
The cable rail at the top of the wall is a guardrail and not an anchorage; keep it its own row here and never tag it to fall protection.
Pile cutoff and whether the wall is left in place or removed are each their own row, with the cutoff elevation on its row.
Utility locating, relocation and de-energizing, and obstruction removal, sit with earthwork; keep them off the wall row.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on two projects in one market, about a dozen proposals from seven or eight bidders; not re-mined.
