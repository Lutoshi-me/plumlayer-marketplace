# Signage

A sign schedule is rarely in the set; build rows off the plans, elevations and the typical code sign details, one row a sign type with its count.
Unit identification is its own row, never folded into room identification, and names its product: tactile plaque, elevated panel, custom off a rendering, or illuminated fixture.
An illuminated unit sign can be bought as a lighting fixture; tag it to signage and electrical both.
Primary power to an illuminated sign is electrical: the circuit, the junction box and the disconnect; signage wires only inside the sign.
Blocking behind wall mounted and dimensional signs goes to rough carpentry; tubes, plates and through bolts behind exterior signs and cabinets go to misc metals.
Room numbering and stenciling is signage; painting does not carry it, so tagging it there leaves it with nobody.
Location class crosses sign function: front of house, back of house, leasing, amenity, garage and pool are rows of their own.
Substrate decides the mount and the mount is priced, so a sign on glass and the same sign on a non-glass wall are two rows.
Photoluminescent stair signage is its own row, apart from the ordinary stair signs.
Dimensional letter sets are one row a word set, apart from panel signage; the wall or panel they mount to comes from casework or millwork.
Exterior building identification is one row a sign with the illumination stated, kept apart from the interior rows.
Monuments and site directionals are rows of their own; their foundations go to concrete or landscaping.
Parking, traffic and garage signage is its own block of rows, clearance bars and crash indicators included.
Fire department connection, fire alarm panel, sprinkler control valve and shutoff valve signs are signage rows, drawn though they are on other trades' sheets.
Elevator jamb plates and floor designation at the elevator entrance are the elevator package; regulatory copy applied as vinyl in the cab is signage.
Vinyl on glass is a row a set rather than a row a sign: address numerals, no smoking legends, elevator regulatory copy.
Panel thickness and print method are an alternate across the whole package, not a split a row.
Epoxy floor and wall wayfinding graphics sit between signage and flooring and neither carries them; tag both.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects, not re-mined.
