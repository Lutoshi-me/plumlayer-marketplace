# Masonry

New work and work on an existing wall are two grains under one division: on new work the wall assembly is the unit, on an existing wall the action taken on it is the unit.
One row per exterior wall assembly code that carries masonry, one per CMU partition type, one per brick mark and one per cast stone mark; an assembly code not found on the plans still gets a row.
Block courses, bond beams and lintel courses collapse to the wall; reinforcing spacing, grout strength, mortar type and joint reinforcement ride the row as attributes.
Room by room and elevation by elevation collapse to the group where the assembly and the mark do not change; brick and block counts ride the mark row as a quantity.
Through wall and base of wall flashing, transition membrane, drip edge, termination bar, weeps and cavity drainage tag masonry; counterflashing, sill pans and metal copings tag roofing.
Air and vapour barrier, weather resistive barrier, dampproofing and waterproofing behind the veneer tag waterproofing, not masonry, on new work.
Joint sealants and caulking at masonry tag the caulking package, never masonry, on new work.
Cast stone and precast bands, sills, caps and copings tag masonry with their anchors and dowels, whoever supplies them.
Thin brick gets rows apart from full bed brick and split again by assembly code; it is a rail kit with its own mortar and wrap. Stone and granite veneer get rows of their own.
Shelf and relieving angles, loose lintels and top of wall restraint clips are furnished by metals and set by the mason; tag both, and tag welding to metals.
Cavity insulation inside a masonry cavity tags masonry, not insulation.
Hollow metal frames in block walls: about half of masons set them; tag frame install to both masonry and the door and frame package.
Firestopping, fire safing and smoke seals are their own package, not masonry's.
Block is left smooth and painted by others; tag painting.
Granite curb, pavers, hardscape and site walls tag sitework; keep site signage masonry as its own row.
On an existing wall, split rows by action: repointing by joint type, brick removal by cause, rebuild apart from replacement, stone replacement by depth, crack repair by substrate, cleaning by method.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects, three new multifamily and one facade restoration, the only one with existing walls; not re-mined.
