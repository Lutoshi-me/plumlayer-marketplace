# Plumbing

Every fixture and equipment mark on a schedule is its own row with its count; accessible toilets are a separate mark from toilets.
Fixtures are scheduled in more than one place. Where a plumbing schedule and an architectural or owner schedule give one mark different products, raise a Question naming both.
Piping enumerates by system, splitting where material changes between residential and commercial or above and below ground; runs, fittings, hangers, sleeves, valves and gauges collapse into the system line.
The pump is plumbing and the pit is not. Tag sump pumps, ejectors and separators to plumbing, the basin, vault, cover, grate, hatch and ladder to concrete.
Tag wiring of plumbing equipment to electrical: float switches, leak shutoffs, pump and aquastat wiring, control panels and starters.
Heat trace is three rows, cable, installation and the insulation over it, each landing with a different party. Tag all three to plumbing and electrical.
Water meters are three rows: main meter supply and install, submeter furnish, submeter install. Add programming where units meter to a data collection system.
The water service pick-up point moves from a blind flange inside to ten feet out. Tag the exterior run to earthwork, and both where the sheet does not mark it.
Shower pan, its waterproofing and the surround land with three parties. Tag pan and waterproofing to plumbing and tiling both; the surround is tiling's, or shower doors' in cast marble.
A sink and its counter are one assembly and two packages. Tag the bowl to plumbing, the cutout and undermount setting to countertops, and both at an undermount bowl.
Appliance connections are one row each: dishwasher, refrigerator and ice maker, washing machine, disposal, gas range. A disposal is furnished by the appliance package and installed by plumbing.
Tag condensate to the discipline that drew it: plumbing on a plumbing sheet, hvac where only a mechanical sheet shows it, both where both do.
Roof penetrations, their flashing, scuppers and metal downspouts are roofing. Tag a storm or overflow drain to plumbing where it carries a tag, and both where it does not.
Excavation, backfill, underslab and foundation drainage go to earthwork, even where the geotechnical sheets or the specification show them on the plumber.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
