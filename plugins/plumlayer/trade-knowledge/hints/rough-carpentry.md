# Rough carpentry

Light gauge partition framing goes to drywall and load-bearing light gauge is its own package; non-bearing partitions drawn wood are rough carpentry.
Blocking inside a wood wall is rough carpentry; blocking in a metal framed wall, grab bars and accessories included, is drywall.
Every blocking type is its own row: in wall accessory, full height at tubs and recessed cabinets, roof, roof edge, curb and sleeper, screen wall, at windows and sills, trim, fire blocking, draft stopping.
Blocking outboard of the sheathing, barrier, gypsum or roof membrane is excluded by framers and by siding both; tag rough carpentry and siding.
Window and exterior door installation is labor with the units furnished by others; tag the install here and to siding, the units to windows.
Split every operation at a window opening: sealant behind the flange, flashing over the flange, pan flashing, sill pans, back dams, backer rod, expansion foam.
Each wall family is its own row: exterior, interior bearing, shear, corridor, non-load-bearing; studs, plates, jacks, kings, headers and posts collapse into it.
Split sheathing by application: wall, shear wall, floor, roof flat, roof pitched, second layer at double sided walls, parapet cap.
Every row carries a furnish and install answer; trusses furnished and trusses set are different rows, and a row saying neither cannot be bid.
Air and vapour barrier at exterior wood walls sits with framing on about half of jobs and with siding on the rest; tag both.
At rooftop equipment, mechanical sets the unit, rough carpentry blocks the curb and roofing flashes it; tag the blocking here, the flashing to roofing.
Blocking for interior trim, shelving, curtain rods and television mounts is excluded by most framers; tag rough carpentry and finish carpentry.
Floor and roof framing systems each get a row: trusses, engineered joists, dimensional joists, engineered beams and headers; member counts collapse into it.
Wood nailers and plates on steel beams are rough carpentry; steel to steel and steel to wood connectors tag structural steel and here both.
Wet set and cast in place anchors and embedment plates are concrete's; epoxy and other post installed anchors are rough carpentry.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four projects in one market, three new build multifamily and one a renovation with no wood framing; not re-mined.
