# Gypsum underlayment

Poured topping and the acoustic mat under it are one package; underlayment product, thickness, compressive strength, mat product and mat thickness each take their own row.
Sealer or primer: this package furnishes the material and flooring installs it; tag the install to flooring, not here.
Perimeter isolation strip: installation tags here, trimming and removal of the excess tags to flooring.
Floor preparation after the pour and before the finish floor goes down tags to flooring, never here.
On a mixed level part wood framed and part slab, only the wood framed portion takes underlayment; split the level and give each substrate its own row.
Stairwells and landings, elevator pits and lobbies, roof levels, cellars, parking, retail and concrete areas each take a row answered yes or no.
Pre-pours at tubs, showers, mechanical closets, chases and dead spaces take their own row with a count, never folded into the poured area.
Mobilization or pour count is a row with the definition of a mobilization on it; pump, hose, mixing and washout collapse into it.
Units, corridors and common areas collapse to one row per assembly; split at corridors only where the assembly itself changes.
Levels collapse to a range where the assembly and the substrate are the same; apartment counts collapse to floor areas, never a row per unit.
Sound mat, isolation foam and tape collapse into the mat row; mix ratio and reinforcing fibers ride the underlayment row.
Sound mat extent does not follow the finish schedule; where the drawings do not say where the mat stops, write the mat row by area and ask.
Measure poured area as gross square feet by level and by building, with no deduction for interior walls or columns.
A floor flatness tolerance the drawings name is its own row; the pour alone does not reach a finish flooring tolerance.
Crack isolation and decoupling membrane under tile tags to tiling over the cured pour; where a tile detail names its own leveling substrate over this floor, tag both.
Acoustic and fire ratings the drawings state ride the rows they govern, keyed to the detail; the resilient channel that co-determines the rating tags to drywall.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on three wood framed multifamily projects in one market; not re-mined.
