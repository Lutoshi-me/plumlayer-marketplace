# Doors, Frames and Hardware

Supply, installation and sales tax are their own rows, never attributes of a product row; a frames row does not say who sets them.
Split by product class first: hollow metal frames, hollow metal leaves, wood doors, prehung assemblies, hardware sets, each its own row.
Hollow metal frames get set by the wall trade before board goes on; tag frame setting to drywall or masonry and keep the frames themselves here.
All doors and frames arrive primed only; tag field paint, stain and six side finishing of prehung leaves to painting, never here.
Aluminum, storefront and curtain wall doors, frames and hardware are glazing's however the door schedule lists them, and balcony and patio doors read the same way.
Casing attached to a prehung assembly rides the door row; casing at hollow metal frames, common area casing and unit base go to carpentry, tag both where the sheet is silent.
Glass in a door goes to glazing, but write it once per place it occurs, at hollow metal openings, at wood doors and at barn or sliding doors, and tag both.
Card readers, power supplies, controllers and low voltage wiring are security's; electrified locksets, strikes, power transfers and smart unit entry locks stay here.
Hardware is counted in sets, but split it again by product category, hinges, locksets, exit devices, closers, pulls, stops, thresholds and gasketing, one row each.
An automatic door operator is four rows, furnish, install, electrical connection and commissioning, and the connection is electrical's.
Fire rating, temperature rise, sound transmission and smoke each get a row; gauge, coating, core and finish ride the product row as attributes.
Count openings and leaves apart: a pair opening is one opening and two leaves, and hardware counts by set against openings.
Overhead, coiling and garage doors are their own package and access doors and panels are mechanical's, however they sit on the schedule.
Frame profile splits rows because it changes who sets the frame, welded, knockdown, nailer slip on at unit entry, thermal break at exterior; the unit entry frame gets its own row.
Split by location wherever the product or the access changes: unit entry, unit interior, corridor, amenity, stairwell, garage, back of house, exterior.

coverage: derived from a book drafted on four multifamily projects in one market for one general contractor; not re-mined.
